# Double critical sibling cloud

This original statistical mechanics problem was inspired by the ensembles and asymptotic fluctuation topics in the [Princeton physics general exam archive](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

The temperature window and entropy detuning leave a two-dimensional Gaussian cloud of typical Gibbs mass. Feasibility truncates the root coordinate and the full path sum. The site increment alone need not be above the site threshold in a typical root. A rare root at its own lower boundary contributes a macroscopic family mass of order one, with independent diffuse sites above the complementary bound. The leading probability of repeated draws from that family comes from its boundary-layer intensity of order N^(-1/2). A beta integral keeps its mass in the Gibbs normalization alongside the cloud.

The oracle integrates in the root coordinate. The reference reverses the order of integration, checks the Gaussian cone's zero-detuning angle, and verifies the rare family's prefactor by integrating its absolute mass explicitly. The shortcut replaces the joint feasibility of ordinary paths by separate cutoffs on the two increments.
