# Author analysis

This original disordered bond lattice is inspired by coupled fluctuation modes and thermal weights in the [Princeton physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

At the critical tilt the independent Gaussian coordinates are x, p and r, with variances gain²*v_A, gain²*(v_B+v_C) and gain²*v_B*v_C/(v_B+v_C). The ordinary feasible region constrains x≥0 and x+p≥0. The column population has extensive entropy surplus ratio*v_C at the tilted Y, so there is no critical column constraint. The tilted covariance terms from pairs sharing a row are suppressed by the positive root margin. Those from pairs sharing a column carry the extensive surplus. The single-bond terms are suppressed by the positive total margin. Negative root or total margins have vanishing counts. Gaussian moments control strips and tails.

Fix a row with unscaled displacement t. Its independent column-plus-bond variables have one pooled critical edge and satisfy p≥0. The contrast r is independent and remains full-line. Its limiting diffuse mass in either setting is exp(−ct) times this tilted cloud. The exact-column collision probability within one selected row vanishes, since a critical individual state has vanishing Gibbs mass in probability. The other rows keep their ordinary masses even while sharing the column disorder, as the covariance bound and each selected row's concentration both hold jointly. The row count contributes phi_A(0)exp(ct)dt after multiplying by sqrt(N).

Fix one actual rare bond with total displacement t. Its scaled count intensity is exp(ct)*phi_A(x)*phi_pool(−x)*phi_contrast(r) on x≥0. At compact strict positive x the selected row's other bonds vanish in conditional mean. Other bonds in the forced column carry an extensive factor exp(−N ratio*v_C) in conditional mean and also vanish. The remaining sample is ordinary. The selected bond adds finite weight exp(−ct+(a_j0−a_j1)x+a_j2*r), where a_j0 and a_j1 are the field tilts after subtracting the window and a_j2 is c times the contrast field. It appears in each denominator. Both outside readouts use the marked ordinary mass. Bounded conditional probabilities over compact windows justify the limits.

The oracle conditions the ordinary cone on x and separates the contrast integrals. The reference conditions on x+p and uses direct energy-displacement integrals. The completed shortcut applies a separate nonnegative cutoff to the ordinary pooled coordinate.
