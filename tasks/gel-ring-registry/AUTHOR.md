# Two filaments grown along a common route

At the fork the two tips have exactly the same reduced path. Their subsequent growth is independent conditional on that location. This makes the three paths a branching random walk on the universal covering tree of the two pores. Summing independent daughter marginals loses the spatial correlation even when the marginal calculation individually includes the entire common lay and its relaxations. For the underlying first-passage factorization see [Lalley, The Local Limit Theorem for Random Walks on Free Groups](https://algo.inria.fr/seminars/sem98-99/lalley.pdf).

For one feed let qⱼ be the one-step probability, including continuation, and Fⱼ the probability of first reaching the adjacent vertex in direction j before stopping. The first step either reaches j or begins an excursion that must return before attempting j again. Thus Fⱼ=qⱼ/[1−Σₖ≠ⱼ qₖ F_reverse(k)]. The probability of eventually stopping at word u is a common constant times the product of these F factors along the unique path to u.

For fixed rooted terminal contours u and v, sum over all possible trunk endpoints t. The summand is the product of three path weights, from the root to t, from t to u, and from t to v, using the three corresponding feeds. The oracle sums this on the finite subtree joining the root, u and v. Every t off that subtree projects to a unique vertex on it. Each edge j farther out contributes F⁰ⱼ F¹_reverse(j) F²_reverse(j). A four-state sum over nonbacktracking excursions accounts for all those side branches. The remaining sum over registered terminal images counts distinct rooted pairs, so the multiplicity of registrations on a periodic contour does not change its weight.

The private calculation instead builds every reduced route of length at most the camera length. It solves three sparse absorbing walks on that tree. At the outer shell, steps leading outside can return to the same shell point, and a fork beyond the shell can send both daughters back. A positive sum over outward shells accounts for exactly those contributions. The validator also repeats the calculation on a larger finite tree with literal absorption outside it for a worked witness. This provides a direct microscopic check independent of the side-branch formula.

All 24 conditional probabilities and winding means agree between the two methods within 10⁻¹⁴. Even the rarest fully sorted and matched configuration has probability above 0.000028. The completed shortcut gives each daughter an independently grown trunk, preserving the correct complete single-ring marginals.
