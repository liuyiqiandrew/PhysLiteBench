# Microcanonical sibling spectroscopy

This original spectroscopy problem draws on the microcanonical ensemble and fluctuation topics in the [Princeton physics general exam archive](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

The ordinary extremes in the fixed-width shell form a Poisson sample with a positive-half Gaussian bridge mark. A sufficiently low parent can contribute several sibling sites at relative order N^(-1/2). Given the parent's displacement its child shell count is Poisson and independent of the background shell count. The exact record weighs falling factorials by the realized combined shell population. A power-Laplace representation of that combined count and the Poisson factorial-moment identity give a single integral after summing over rare parent energies. Conditioning on a nonempty shell uses the ordinary limiting occurrence probability.

The reference integrates in the Poisson generating-function coordinate and checks the result against direct Poisson count summation for a finite parent window. The shortcut uses the mean shell population with Poisson factorial moments instead of the realized denominator.
