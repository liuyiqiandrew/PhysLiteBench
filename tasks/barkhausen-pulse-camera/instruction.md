Two magnetic walls scan the same pinned strip in opposite directions on separate resets. Each stops when its forward velocity first vanishes. The experiment resolves both stopping distances, then reads two optical speed gates in the region visited by both scans. Predict the conditional fraction passing the second gate among samples passing the first.

Fit the spatial pinning diffusivity and implement Model in /app/model.py. The public README gives the shared force law, stopping rule, physical velocities, examples, and grading tolerances.
