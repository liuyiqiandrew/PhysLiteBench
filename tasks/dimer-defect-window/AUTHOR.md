# Dimer defect window, revision 1

A parity-sensitive plate measures correlations of hard dimers on an open triangular lattice. An immobile vacancy removes a site before the sheet equilibrates. Binding exclusion persists around the new faces. A four-site rhombus has two coverings with weights 1 and (zd)², so each one-bond calibration reading identifies the diagonal activity z. Seed 43115 chooses the optical probes and defect preparations. The 0.003 probability tolerance is much larger than the uncertainty from rounding and the discrepancy between the two exact calculations.

The oracle traces the faces of the actual punctured graph. It reverses arrows along a spanning tree of the dual graph until each bounded face has an odd number of clockwise arrows. Then every term in the Pfaffian of the weighted skew adjacency K has the same sign. Reversing all marked weights multiplies a covering by its observed parity sign. The optical probability is half of one minus the resulting signed Pfaffian ratio. Solving with K on the marked endpoints and taking a Pfaffian of order at most twelve computes the ratio, including for marks that share an endpoint. [Fendley, Moessner and Sondhi](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0206159)

The private reference orients the intact sheet with all horizontal arrows positive, vertical signs alternating between columns, and opposite signs on the diagonals. Each removed vertex sends a disorder line horizontally to the exterior. Reversing crossed arrows restores the face condition up to orientation changes at vertices. It computes joint occupations by removing both endpoints of each forced bond and evaluating positive partition functions from sparse determinants. The finite expansion of the product of (1−2n) over marked edges then gives the odd probability. A third method counts even and odd matchings by direct recursion on every small fixture. The oracle and private reference agree on all forty measurements within 2 × 10⁻⁹ at the fitted activity.

The completed physical shortcut allows outside bonds to bind independently to each boundary site of a local patch. It treats the resulting probe occupations independently. The complete four-site calibration is exact. The patch forgets the constraints on surrounding sites and the parity correlations between marks.
