# Author analysis

This original controlled-disorder calorimeter takes inspiration from the Boltzmann and Gaussian ensemble problems in the [Princeton physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams). Two families are deliberately held at finite displacement from the ordinary family extreme and contribute Gibbs mass in every sample.

The ordinary mass B is the tilted Gaussian integral on x≥0 and x+y≥0, with tilt c fields[j]−window. Compact negative prefix margins are absent. Counts of ordinary configurations with positive prefix margins concentrate because two samples sharing a prefix have an exponentially small relative covariance determined by that prefix margin. First moments control Gaussian tails and boundary strips. For each fixed tag, the descendant cloud instead uses y≥0, with coefficients exp(−c tag_energies[j]) and tilt c tag_fields[j]−window. Both tagged diffuse masses add to the ordinary mass in the normalization Z.

The expectation of a repeated tag site in a finite unscaled displacement interval is a sum over single forced labels. Its sqrt(N)-scaled count density is phi_L(0) exp(cd) dd. The forced label has limiting weight W=exp(−c(tag_energies[tag]+d)); its other siblings, the opposite tag, and the ordinary tree converge conditionally to the same ordinary masses. For two draws return W²/(Z+W)² in the conditional integrand. An opposite draw multiplies it by the clipped mass of the opposite tag divided by Z+W. The site-window interval is compact, so the scaled intensity can be integrated against the bounded conditional probability.

The oracle evaluates the ordinary tilted cone and uses the mass-share variable W/(Z+W) to integrate the finite site interval. The reference independently integrates the swapped Gaussian coordinates and the raw displacement. The calibrated shortcut uses the same three populations but leaves the forced labelled site out of the Gibbs denominators.
