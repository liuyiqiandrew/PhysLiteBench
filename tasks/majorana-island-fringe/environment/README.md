# Ramsey readout of a superconducting island

An island carries 2n Majorana modes, labelled by Hermitian operators γ₀ through γ₂ₙ₋₁ with {γⱼ,γₖ}=2δⱼₖ. Set ℏ and k_B to one. For real orthonormal vectors u,v define the pair parity Q(u,v)=−iγ(u)γ(v), where γ(u)=∑uⱼγⱼ. Ordered pairs retain their supplied orientation. Write Qⱼ=−iγ₂ⱼγ₂ⱼ₊₁. The preparation is thermal equilibrium at inverse temperature `beta` for H=−∑ EⱼQⱼ/2 within the specified eigenvalue `parity` of ∏Qⱼ. Here Eⱼ=1+`ripple` cos(2π(j+1/2)/n), j=0,…,n−1. The preparation Hamiltonian is switched off during readout.

Two independent qubits, an arm qubit and a meter, start in (|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2, where Z|0⟩=|0⟩. The arm selects one of the two supplied pulse lists, `arms[0]` or `arms[1]`, in chronological order. A pulse [a,b,θ,κ] applies the integrated Hamiltonian (θ+κ Z_meter)Q(uₐ,uᵦ)/2 to the island and meter. Each pulse is square. Its duration is included in θ and κ. All scalar offsets and all Hamiltonians between pulses vanish. The same coherent meter stays coupled to the apparatus through both alternatives. There is no intermediate measurement.

The input `frame` sets the contact vectors for all pulses. For `site` the vectors are the standard unit vectors. For `wave` they are rows of the real orthogonal cosine matrix of order d=2n. Row zero is constant 1/√d. Its other entries are uₐⱼ=√(2/d) cos[πa(j+1/2)/d] for a=1,…,d−1.

At the end, measure the meter along cos(φ)X+sin(φ)Y with φ=`meter_phase`, and retain the specified outcome `outcome` (±1). On the retained shots measure cos(λ)X+sin(λ)Y on the arm, λ=`phase`. Use the usual Pauli convention ⟨0|Y|1⟩=−i. The reported dimensionless signal is the conditional arm expectation times the same unknown positive visibility V in [0.65,0.95]. Measurements of the island itself are never required. All inputs have meter acceptance probability at least 0.05.

The integer n lies in [3,32], beta in [0.4,6] in inverse energy units, and ripple in [−0.45,0.45]. Each pulse list has at most 18 entries, 0≤a,b<2n, a≠b, |θ|≤2π and |κ|≤π. Phases are in radians. The empty pulse list is valid. For example, with the meter uncoupled, meter_phase=0, outcome=+1 and phase=0, one pulse [0,1,2π,0] on arm 1 and an empty arm 0 give −V. Pair parity has eigenvalues ±1, so this pulse multiplies either island state by −1.

Implement `Model.fit(records)` returning self and setting `self.visibility`. Each row of `data/calibration.json` contains an `input` and a `value` rounded to eight decimal places. The calibration arms follow identical histories. `Model.predict(inputs)` returns a finite real NumPy array of shape (len(inputs),), including empty inputs. Absolute calibration errors must be below 0.000002 and the maximum absolute signal error in each family of fresh experiments must be below 0.003. The starter replaces each pair parity by its thermal mean during a pulse. It supplies a working visibility fit.
