# Majorana island fringe, revision 6

This compact instrument uses charge transfer between an island and two normal dots to modify a later Ramsey fringe. A cyclic quantum register persists while a sequence of distinguishable dots is reset. A controlled shift writes the tag, a separate marker carries any available timing information, and a coherent dot analyzer erases the local record. The final clock selection lets different histories interfere. Reusing the island retains its correlations with the clock. There are 12 calibration rows and 24 held-out preparations across three groups. The solve and verifier allowances are 1,800 and 180 seconds.

For a fixed incoming counter k let B be the column of two island operators obtained by evolving the initial dot state through its tunnel contacts and charging dwells and projecting onto each outgoing one-electron dot basis vector. The public single-encounter routine produces B with the fermionic signs retained. The two conditional operators are L₁(k)=⟨erase|tag⟩ tag†B and L₀(k)=erase†B−L₁(k). A postselected analyzer removes the empty and doubly occupied outgoing dot sectors. These sectors can contribute during each tunnel sequence.

Represent the joint state by D² island operator blocks ρₖₗ. For ket tag u and bra tag v, contribute Lᵤ(k)ρₖₗLᵥ(l)† to block r,s where r=k+u mod D and s=l+v mod D. Multiply by ⟨mᵥ|mᵤ⟩ and by exp[−iχ(r(r−1)−s(s−1))/2] for the counter dwell. The final counter projection contracts the full density matrix with its specified analyzer. The Ramsey numerator is the real part of exp(−iλ)Tr(U₀†U₁ρ_island), divided by the retained trace.

The oracle evolves this joint density built from a finite Jordan–Wigner representation of the at most six addressed island Majoranas. The independent hidden reference explicitly enumerates tag strings and initial register amplitudes. It expands the fermionic gates as signed polynomials in Majorana monomials, contracts the dots analytically at each encounter and weights every pair of histories by their marker inner products. Initial island expectations come from a separate fermionic Wick recurrence with a total-parity insertion. It uses neither the public dot helper nor the oracle’s density update.

Resolved-count preparations use orthogonal markers. Coherent-tally preparations preserve marker overlap, and feedback preparations include a coherent clock input, analyzer and charge-dependent tunnel phases. A control that adds a perfect unobserved tag record reproduces the resolved-count group and fails the coherent groups. The independent-shot starter fails all three. The standalone validator checks both controls, all retained probabilities and the public cancellation example. Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/validate_majorana_island_fringe.py` to reproduce the checks. Add `--generate` to write the stored fixtures.
