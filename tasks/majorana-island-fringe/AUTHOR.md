# Majorana island fringe, revision 7

Coherent coupling between a charge dot and Majorana modes can change the state of a superconducting island. This synthetic apparatus combines such contacts with a shared charge counter and a spin-polarized Fermi lead. The lead never exchanges particles with the island. A temporary local potential records each dot tag in the same lead, and an optional regional charge measurement conditions the final Ramsey signal. The 12 calibration rows fit one visibility. The 24 held-out preparations cover an unobserved lead, a meter reporting zero charge transfer and a meter reporting nonzero transfer. The solve and verifier allowances are 1,800 and 180 seconds. For the charge-transfer background, see [Flensberg, Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 090503 (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.090503).

For each incoming counter value k let B be the two outgoing one-electron dot amplitudes. Projecting the tagged and complementary branches onto the analyzer gives L₁(k)=⟨erase|tag⟩ tag†B and L₀(k)=erase†B−L₁(k). The public helper keeps both operators, the counter's cyclic shift and dwell, and the complex overlap of every fresh marker. It traces the prepared fixed-parity island using at most six addressed Majoranas. It returns raw island traces for each bra and ket history, including the final counter projection.

In the sector with N particles a single-particle evolution U induces its Nth exterior power. Between site occupation sets I,J, its matrix element is det U[I,J]. Lifting the orbital eigenbasis and weighting each occupied set by its Boltzmann factor prepares the canonical thermal state ρ. For tag string a, multiply the one-body quenches in chronological order and lift that product to the N-electron unitary Vₐ. An unobserved lead multiplies each history pair by Tr(VᵦρVₐ†). For a meter reading d the multiplier is ∑ₖ Tr(Pₖ₊d VᵦPₖρPₖ Vₐ†), where Pₖ retains all configurations with that regional total. This expression includes the disturbance of the initial count measurement. Normalize the weighted arm coherence by the separately weighted probability and apply the specified quadrature and visibility. The determinant identity is reviewed in [Klich, Quantum Noise in Mesoscopic Physics (2003)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0209642).

The independent reference builds each occupation-basis many-body Hamiltonian directly, including the fermion sign of a hop across the ring boundary. It exponentiates the many-body matrices, computes the regional measurement blocks and traces them. A separate Clifford-polynomial expansion follows the dot and island histories and evaluates their initial thermal moments by a Wick recurrence. The reference uses neither public history tables nor exterior-power gates.

The starter disconnects the lead. A second physics control measures every site before the first regional readout. It reproduces the group with no charge meter, and it loses coherence between equal-count configurations in both charge-resolved groups. The validator regenerates all rows with an independent comparison and checks positivity, normalization across all allowed charge changes, and the analytic four-site witness.
