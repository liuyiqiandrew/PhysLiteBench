For the current filter, tilt the clockwise and counterclockwise rates to the selected net rate. Eliminate the three fast states with the Poisson inverse of their generator before multiplying the screw derivatives; their order matters. For a difference of starting phases keep the initial-state corrector in both the conditional numerator and acceptance.

Closing the full frame requires the diagonal rotation kernel. Compute its trace with the two gates in both parity sectors on SU(2). The central sign flip acts as +1 on integer angular momenta and −1 on half-integer angular momenta. Add or subtract the resulting traces before forming the selected quotient.

For consecutive director returns conditional on A and B, multiply the local kernel from U to UA by the kernel from UA to UAB before integrating over the initial frame U. Both gates and any spectator-spin marker belong in this same average. The zero-wave endpoint weights use the ordinary rigid-top heat kernel.
