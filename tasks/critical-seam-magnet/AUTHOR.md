# Two magnetic films on reversible seams

The films are conditionally independent given both seam polarities. For a physical seam state s, let Zᵢ(s) be the partition function relative to the welded film and let mᵢ(s) be the spin-product mean relative to its welded mean. The switches are sampled with weights proportional to exp[g(h·s+c sₓsᵧ)] Z₁(s)Z₂(s). The overlap is the resulting average of m₁m₂. Subtracting the product of the two averages gives the connected signal. Multiplying the covariance between m₁m₂ and the selected polarity by g gives the dial response. A finite difference of the overlap checks the latter on every response setting.

## Continuum spin sectors

Place each site at z=x+i × aspect × y. For neutral charges eᵢ=±1 at all marked sites, let W(e)=∏ᵢ<ⱼ |θ₁(zᵢ−zⱼ)|^(eᵢeⱼ/2). The square of a spin structure amplitude Bν is the sum over charges of W(e) |θν(Σeᵢzᵢ/2)|². Period-one theta functions use modulus i times the aspect. Factors common to the marked amplitudes cancel in the measurement. The unmarked amplitudes are |θν(0)|. Relative to the fully welded film, the signs for (ξₓ,ξᵧ)=(++,+−,−+,−−) are ++++, −−++, −++− and −+−+ respectively for ν=1,2,3,4. The observable in each sector uses its own partition normalization. For the critical torus correlation formula and the relation to physical disorder lines, see [Bayraktaroğlu and Izyurov, equations 2.1, 4.3 and 4.10](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11324v1). [Di Francesco, Saleur and Zuber](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(87)90202-1) give an earlier derivation.

## Finite physical checks

The independent calculation uses the microscopic square lattice. In the dual low-temperature loop expansion, a marked spin product changes the sign of a domain wall each time it crosses a string joining a pair of marked spins. Draw the strings inside the cut rectangle. On the directed dual-edge graph, forbid immediate reversals and assign each continuation a weight (√2−1) exp(i turn/2), including the string signs and the two chosen winding signs. The square root of the Kac–Ward determinant sums those signed loops. Combining the four choices selects the physical winding classes. The empty doubly periodic fermion determinant is zero at criticality. Direct summation over all 4,096 spin assignments on a 3 × 4 torus independently verifies the marked and unmarked sectors, including negative marked traces to within 10⁻¹².

Using 64 columns gives every measurement to within 0.000072. Extrapolation from 32 and 64 columns agrees with the continuum calculation to within 0.00000063. The oracle computes the continuum values directly and does not use lattice extrapolation. The completed physical shortcut resolves both films' exact marked and unmarked critical traces, but gives them independently equilibrated seam switches. It misses the overlap, connected and response groups by 0.536, 0.295 and 0.254 respectively, against tolerance 0.004. Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/validate_critical_seam_magnet.py` to reproduce the checks.
