# Two-atom spin-gas interferometry

Impenetrable bosons preserve their rank when they hop locally. The Raman transfer is nonlocal and can change that rank. The two beams and the rotation plate act on one spin configuration. For a fixed pair of initial and final charge configurations, contract each rotation from its source rank to its destination rank. Every index belongs to the same prepared Markov chain. The all-up optical reference sums the same positive charge coherences with unit spin overlap.

The oracle contracts a two-state tensor network against the common Markov law. Its charge weights are minors of the reduced Gaussian likelihood matrix. The private reference diagonalizes the open hopping chain and uses a different block determinant with an occupied-site minor and a vacant-site complement. It then enumerates every spectator configuration and every ordered spin list with explicit single-spin amplitudes. The two routines agree to 5e−10 across 24 held-out readings. The validator also checks positivity of every resolved charge weight, exchange of addressing beam order, and the two elementary consecutive-address limits.

The completed shortcut treats the spin permutations faithfully within each cycle, including the exact Markov marginals after skipping intermediate ranks. It draws a new spin chain for each cycle. This loses correlations between different cycles in the same exposure. Its maximum discrepancies in the disjoint, nested and interlaced groups are 0.1444, 0.0090 and 0.0574. The tolerance is 0.004. The public starter instead keeps internal labels attached to atom identities. The ordinary blank calibration is equally informative for each model.
