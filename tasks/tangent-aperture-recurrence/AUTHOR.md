# Author analysis

This original tangent spectrometer is inspired by energy windows and Gaussian fluctuations in the [Princeton physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

In a setting the ordinary mass is the critical tilted Gaussian on x≥0 with y≥curvature*(x−center)²−x. It follows by concentration on strict positive prefix margins and Gaussian control of the tails and changing aperture boundary. A marked ordinary mass clips this region by its y band.

At fixed finite address u=N^(1/4)(x−center) and line t=√N*(x+y), the two finite-size aperture conditions are exactly t≥curvature_j*(u−shift_j)². The count density after multiplying by N^(3/4) is phi_F(center)*phi_L(−center)*exp(ct) du dt. At positive center, the other sites of the conditioned family contribute vanishing expected normalized weight. At center zero and u>0 the root displacement is positive with magnitude of order N^(1/4), again suppressing its other sites. At center zero and u<0 those other sites instead dominate both denominators because a positive-y band remains admitted in each setting. Thus only u≥0 contributes when the center is zero. The thin address margin has vanishing measure.

For each surviving label the selected site's weight in setting j is exp(−ct+(a_j0−a_j1)*center), with a_ji=c*field_ji−window_j. Add that weight to its ordinary denominator. A marked outside draw uses the corresponding ordinary y band. Compact address and displacement intervals allow bounded convergence after conditioning on the selected label.

The oracle integrates all admissible address lengths at fixed t and computes the bulk conditional on x. The reference conditions the bulk on y and integrates along u first. The completed shortcut retains negative microscopic addresses at center zero, ignoring the diverging diffuse mass in their conditioned family.
