## Derivation

Write Γ(W) for the number-conserving Fock-space action of a passive single-particle matrix W. Define D(λ) with diagonal entries exp(iλ) on the bank and one elsewhere. Ignoring the first count while retaining its phase exp(−iλq) amounts to applying D(−λ) to the state averaged over a common bank rotation D(φ), with φ uniform on [0,2π]. This preserves every coherence between configurations with the same bank total.

For the herald, write Z for the diagonal sign change on its rails. Its retained projector is [1+sΓ(Z)]/2 for parity s. Let U and V be the before and after meshes. The two density-matrix arms contribute matrices

    Kᵢⱼ = U† Zⁱ V† D(λ) V Zʲ U D(−λ),    i,j ∈ {0,1}.

Sum their expectations with weights sⁱ⁺ʲ/4 and divide by the same expression at λ=0. Without a herald only i=j=0 enters with unit weight. The fringe is the rotated real part at the calibrated visibility. These expressions follow by expanding both projectors in the sequential Born rule.

The oracle purifies each thermal rail of occupation n with a second rail. Its pair amplitude before squeezing is t=√[n/(1+n)]. After the physical squeezer, the pair matrix for the physical and auxiliary rails has entries B₁₁=−exp(iχ)tanh r, B₁₂=B₂₁=t/cosh r and B₂₂=−B₁₁* t². Passive preparation transforms B into PBPᵀ, with P extended by identity on the auxiliary modes. The squared vacuum amplitude of the prepared pure state is c=∏[(1+n)cosh r]⁻¹. If C is its pair matrix after a bank phase rotation and C′ is obtained by applying K to it, the Gaussian overlap is

    c exp[−½ Σⱼ log(1−zⱼ)],    zⱼ = eigenvalues of C†C′.

The eigenvalues have modulus below one. Choosing each logarithm continuously from zero fixes the phase even when the determinant of the product crosses the negative real axis. The oracle averages 64 equally spaced bank phases.

## Revision 5, frequency-entangled photon transfer

Each new source emits a photon into each input rail. Its normalized joint spectral amplitude is Gaussian in the ordered input frequencies and can carry quadratic phase and relative delays. A coherent marker can tag two candidate emissions. A dispersive plate in a single internal rail gives a frequency-dependent phase to a photon only if the spatial interferometer actually sends it through that rail. The parity meter and both number detectors remain insensitive to frequency. Photon exchange rules for correlated spectral inputs are formulated in [Shchesnovich, Physical Review A 91, 013844 (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.91.013844).

The oracle contracts output creation and annihilation operators. Each permutation σ matches the bra photon j with ket photon σ(j), forcing their frequencies to agree. Write Tₘ(x) for the one-photon network, including the plate and either identity or gate-parity sign, indexed by m. Projecting the many-photon gate uses the *same* parity sign choice for every photon on a history. Each trace term is a spectral integral of Fₐ(x)* Fᵦ(x with permuted labels) times the product of entries [Tₘ(xⱼ)† exp(iλP_bank) Tₙ(xⱼ)]ⱼ,σⱼ. The source weights are cₐ* cᵦ ⟨dₐ|dᵦ⟩. Remove the known entrance phase, take the requested real quadrature and divide by the accepted mass.

For each photon, its plate phase on the ket minus that on the bra has integer coefficient −1, 0 or 1. Each remaining frequency integral is a complex Gaussian with a positive real quadratic form R and an imaginary part H. After whitening H with R, the square root of the determinant is obtained by multiplying the individual roots of 1+iλⱼ for the whitened eigenvalues. This preserves its phase as the chirp is continuously switched on.

The independent reference forms a symmetrized spatial–frequency wavefunction on frequency quadrature nodes. For each input-label assignment it propagates every photon up to the plate. It applies the plate frequency by frequency in the occupied rail, projects the total rail parity, and then applies the final mesh on the tensor product of spatial rails. It sums output probabilities over all unresolved frequencies and spatial counts. Two orthogonal marker components implement its partial trace. This method uses no formula for Gaussian exchange integrals.
