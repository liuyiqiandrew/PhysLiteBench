# Few-photon Raman scattering with a single saturable emitter

The calibrated thermal counter measures how many photons moved across a polarization bank. The new input is an exact two-photon Fock state. When two photons share a single Λ atom, absorption occupies the only excited state, emission can change the ground state, and the order of interactions affects subsequent amplitudes. A final temporal-mode sort and ground-state projection recover interference between these physical histories. All 30 waveguide shots are held out. The worked pair is separate. The thermal calibration fixes V=0.8436. Continuous-mode Fock-state input–output theory for multilevel systems is developed by [Baragiola et al., Phys. Rev. A 86, 013811 (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.86.013811).

## Oracle and independent reference

The oracle contracts the propagator between unnormalized coherent input and output test fields. Give the two incoming modes separate variables x₀,x₁ and the two outgoing bras separate variables y₀,y₁. For input field f and output bra h the generator is the free no-emission atomic generator plus ∑c(h_c L_c − L_c† f_c + h_c f_c I). Retaining the coefficient of x₀x₁y₀y₁ gives an exact Fock amplitude. Higher powers cannot contribute. There are 16 coefficients, each a three-component atomic state. The waveform coefficients are constant on each interval, so a matrix exponential gives its exact transfer. The pulse rotates every occupied coefficient at its specified boundary. The two polarization labels of the temporal receivers are resolved at the end and their intensities are weighted by the transfer fringe.

The private reference expands in physical absorption and emission times. Both incoming photons can pass directly, one can excite the atom while the other passes through, or both can excite the atom in two strictly ordered, disjoint dwell intervals. For each possible assignment, a lower bidiagonal ODE integrates the ordered event times through the prescribed detuning changes and pulse. The largest block dimension is 15. Direct photons each contribute their field overlap. Summing all histories coherently on the final atomic ket before the atomic measurement recovers the same amplitudes. The reference does not import the coherent-field oracle or its apparatus routines.

Two Raman flips have amplitude exp(−2) and return the emitter to g₀. The validator checks the four amplitudes through the public fringe and acceptance. It also checks invariance under subdividing an interval without changing the continuous envelopes and under globally rephasing a source photon.

## Controls

The public starter exactly solves isolated one-photon scattering but imposes an artificial order on the two incident photons with the spin pulse between their passages. The stronger negative control uses the full interacting scattering process and adds an unobserved projective measurement of g₀, g₁ or e at the pulse boundary, destroying coherence the actual RF pulse preserves. Both controls calibrate the visibility, finish, and produce finite readings. The frozen values are generated with the ordered-dwell reference. `scripts/validate_photon_transfer_fringe.py` compares both methods on every reading and runs both controls. The hidden verifier uses only the frozen data.
