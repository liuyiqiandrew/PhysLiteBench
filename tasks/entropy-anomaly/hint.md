The Lorentz force does no mechanical work, but it rotates the velocity moments that carry heat through the temperature gradient. Keep the finite-mass calorimetric balance before taking the mass to zero. The four cubic velocity moments vx^3, vx^2*vy, vx*vy^2 and vy^3 mix under the Lorentz force and have distinct dissipative rates under anisotropic drag. Subtract their parts fixed by the local Gaussian first and second moments, solve the remaining coupled cubic-moment balance, and use it in the kinetic energy flux. A magnetic positional mobility alone does not determine this heat flux. At zero field recover the anisotropic kinetic term; at uniform temperature recover ordinary force-work dissipation.
