# Spectral-hole echo conditioned on acoustic emissions

Discrete bistable defects cause dephasing in solids. The pocket geometry, optical slits and color-resolved emission monitor specify the benchmark's idealized apparatus. [Paladino et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 88, 228304 (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.228304)

For one pocket write lambda for its Poisson mean, c = eta gamma for the registered flip rate per defect and T for the listening duration. A realization of n defects contributes a factor exp(−ncT) for the absence of additional emissions. This changes its Poisson mean to m = lambda exp(−cT) and leaves an overall weight exp(m−lambda). Each defect's free polarity has flip rate gamma−c during the window and rate gamma afterward. An assigned emission at a known time additionally reverses that source defect.

In the oracle the pocket's ordinary and Ramsey-weighted Fourier transforms share the same construction. The quiet background gives exp[m(g−1)] where g is the marked transform of a single, freely evolving defect. For a detected click of color k, the additional marked transform is mc p(k) times the transform of one source with that flip imposed. For two detections, distinct sources give m²c² p(k)p(l) times the product of their respective marked transforms. When their colors match there is another term mc² p(k) times the transform of a single defect carrying both flips. These factors follow by counting labeled sources at fixed occupancy before summing over n. The source assignments are summed over pockets and domains using raw intensities. The collected count and echo are divided only after optical selection. The complete shortcut drops the same-source contribution.

The private reference fixes the integer occupancies, enumerates choices of one or two actual emitters, and sums the telegraph histories of the remaining ordinary defects using multinomial histograms. Given initial pocket sum x and a history coefficient H per defect, the accumulated phase has mean x sum(H)/n and variance sigma² [sum(H²)−sum(H)²/n]. Linear algebra classifies every attainable optical gate for each admissible occupied-pocket pattern. Two constraints leave a one-dimensional Gaussian quadrature when all three pockets are occupied. The private calculation uses the original Poisson prior and the acoustic likelihood exp(−ncT)c^k directly.

In the worked example, at fixed overlap occupancy n, retained same-source assignments have multiplicity n and red probability 0.6. Distinct sources have multiplicity n(n−1) and color probability 0.6² or 0.6×0.4. The kick Stark sum after two registered flips is exactly x for a repeated source. For distinct sources it is Gaussian with mean x(1−4/n) and variance 4 sigma²(2−4/n). The validator sums these expressions independently of either general solver.

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/validate_defect_spectral_hole.py` to reproduce the labels, example and control. The public starter solves the optical preparation exactly with an unobserved acoustic channel. The complete wrong-physics control includes emission backaction, the color-resolved intensities and all no-click factors but assigns each click to a different defect.
