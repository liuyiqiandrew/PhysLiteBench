# Two-excitation spectral-hole memory

Collective spin-wave writes and mode-selective optical reads are the basis of ensemble quantum memories. This idealized device combines two collective excitations with classically fluctuating site shifts and a two-coordinate spectral preparation. The memory level does not control the classical strain flip rate. [Duan, Lukin, Cirac and Zoller, Nature 414, 413 (2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0105105) and [Paladino et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 88, 228304 (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.228304)

Two spin raising operators give the unordered pair i,j the coefficient w1_i w2_j + w1_j w2_i, with i≠j. Double occupation vanishes. Retrieval uses the conjugate of the same exchange combination for the specified retrieval modes. For each pair of amplitudes A_h and A_k*, the oracle averages exp(−i l·F) over all telegraph signs on their union of sites. A site common to the amplitudes contributes its signed difference between the two storage phases. At fixed counts and accepted optical reading the conditional F is Gaussian, giving exp(−i l·mean − l·covariance·l/2). Summing all pairs gives the two powers and their cross term. The physical control discards coherence between different occupied pairs but keeps exchange within every pair, both optical slits and the two arms' shared strain.

The private reference enumerates literal n-site two-level Hilbert states and applies the two raising operators to a ground-state vector. It enumerates the joint telegraph process and integrates the positive output intensities and complex interference over the unresolved Gaussian coordinates by Gauss–Hermite quadrature. Its independently calculated optical weights use a least-squares solution for independent unit normals and direct sums over every ordered pocket assignment with the required total site count. A line carries one power of the slit width and a smooth background carries two. All allowed two-level operators are local to a memory site.

For the worked optical line the conditional three couplings are exchangeable with means 0.7/3 and covariance 0.8² times the projector perpendicular to (1,1,1). If P is the storage phase on one chosen pair, and D the phase difference between two distinct pairs, the balanced-port fringe is 2 Re[exp(i 0.32) E(exp(−iP))] / [1+(3+6 Re E(exp(−iD)))/9]. Summing the eight possible single-kick sign vectors gives the stated example.
