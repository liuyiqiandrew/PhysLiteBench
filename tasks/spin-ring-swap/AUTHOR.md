# Conditional spin exchange at criticality

The ordinary exchange measurement is a Rényi overlap. Compared with the finite-temperature, few-site probe in spin-ring-fringe, this device retains extensive arcs in the critical scaling limit and measures how their exchange is correlated with a coarse or overlapping parity record in the unexchanged complement. A rotation of one ring can change the conditional exchange without changing any record probability. The physical string correction for separated spin blocks was derived for XY chains by Fagotti and Calabrese. [J. Stat. Mech. P04016 (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/2010/04/P04016)

First resolve the relative gap parities s_j in the m−1 interior gaps. A physical spin exchange moves equal total magnetization between the two copies in every contributing matrix element. Thus the exterior gap sign in the exchange numerator must equal the product of the interior signs, although all gap records are possible in the input. The cumulative interior signs give the relative Jordan–Wigner string of the exchanged blocks. Multiplying them by the phase plates gives arc signs t_l. The projectors in the m−1 interior gaps are a sum over subsets H of gaps with coefficient equal to the product of s_j over H. Diagonalizing the two replica labels gives a conjugate pair of single-particle phase symbols. The first symbol is +1 in the exterior gap, i t_l in arc l, and −1 in each gap of H, with +1 in other gaps. The resulting replica trace is the absolute square of the filled-orbital determinant of that symbol. The exact acceptance probability follows by the analogous parity projectors on the recorded gaps.

Each boundary phase jump is ±π/2 along the shortest direction on the unit circle. Denote its sign by e_i. In a balanced sector, sum e_i=0, squared-determinant asymptotics give the chord product ∏_(i<j) [2 sin π(x_j−x_i)]^(e_i e_j/4). If the phase winds, the required neutral representation uses a jump of greater absolute magnitude and is subleading. Both results follow from the Fisher–Hartwig representation of sign and phase symbols. Lattice factors cancel against the product of single-arc swap calibrations, each with geometric factor [2 sin π(b−a)]^(−1/4). Any nonempty union of gaps has vanishing parity expectation in the limit, so all full gap records have equal incoming probability. Sum the exchange contributions consistent with the actual records and divide by their combined acceptance. [Deift, Its and Krasovsky, Ann. Math. 174, 1243 (2011)](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2011/174-2/p12)

For two unrotated arcs let x=sin π(b−a) sin π(d−c) / [sin π(c−a) sin π(d−b)]. With no gap record the ratio is (1−x)^(−1/4) [1+sqrt(x)+sqrt(1−x)]/2. With just the interior gap sign + or −, replace the square-bracket factor divided by two by 1+sqrt(x) or sqrt(1−x), respectively. These independent expressions check the public example.

The finite reference constructs the actual half-filled orbital matrices for all replica twists and all acceptance projectors. Separately, a literal spin calculation builds the positive XX ground-state amplitudes in the occupation basis, conditions on each gap parity sector and contracts the rotated spin density matrices without using any fermionic swap. The validator checks agreement at eight sites and independently extrapolates finite rings for the hidden records. The complete control retains every singly monitored parity marginal of the exchange signal and assumes the different gap signs are independent.
