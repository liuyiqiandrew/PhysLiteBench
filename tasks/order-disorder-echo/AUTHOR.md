# Order and disorder echo

The ring is the critical transverse-field Ising chain in physical spin boundary conditions. Its two pulses correspond to a pair of order fields and a pair of dual disorder fields. Individual dipoles have the same even-vacuum two-point function. Their products differ through the sign of the energy-density channel in the disorder-field operator product. The same vacuum dipole normalizations are used when preparing the lowest odd spin state.

The continuum solution evaluates the Ising conformal blocks on the cylinder in the pair fusion basis. It sums over assignments of one endpoint of each pulse to each of two sets, then resolves the channels with a finite character transform. For each dual pair in the fermion channel the full nonchiral block acquires a minus sign. In the odd ground state an additional spin pair at opposite temporal infinities implements the magnetic sector. Its finite cross ratio with a dipole depends on that dipole's length. The positive Euclidean filter selects the initial sheet for real-time continuation. The code follows the analytic block and its slope through any nearby zeros, including when evolution reverses. The many-point block construction is given by [Ardonne and Sierra](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2863).

The fixture calculation uses finite microscopic spin rings. Bond dipoles and transverse strings become distinct ordered Majorana products. Contractions use the exact dispersion and complex-time phase, with antiperiodic modes in the physical even sector and periodic modes with an occupied uniform zero fermion in the odd sector. Wick contraction is a signed Pfaffian. Averaging the two lattice placements adjacent to the target dual endpoints removes their half-site offset. Extrapolation from N=128 and 192 gives the private fixtures. N=64 and 128 provide an independent refinement check. The worst difference in the reported quadrature from the analytic answer is 0.0000033, against tolerance 0.004. Direct diagonalization of the eight-spin periodic Hamiltonian in its physical Pauli basis checks both complex sector amplitudes, including the sign of the imaginary part.

The completed shortcut evaluates the full sector and evolution but treats each dual pulse as an ordinary order dipole. At the fitted visibility its maximum errors are 0.861, 2.195 and 1.750 in the even, odd and reversed-evolution sets. Choosing a new principal square root after each evolution loses the continuation of real-time paths and gives errors up to 0.780. Run `python scripts/validate_order_disorder_echo.py` to check frozen controls and the literal Pauli-chain witness. `--full` also recomputes the large microscopic rings, and `--write` regenerates the data.
