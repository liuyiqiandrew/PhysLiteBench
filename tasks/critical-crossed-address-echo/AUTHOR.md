# Critical crossed address echo

This original three-coordinate statistical model was inspired by the thermodynamic limits and conditioned ensembles in the [Princeton physics general exam archive](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

The ordinary Gibbs cloud is a tilted Gaussian truncated by the two marginal entropy bounds and the full path bound. Selecting both a row and a column in boundary layers of width N^(-1/2) gives an order N^(-1) event. Conditional on those two rare configurations, the total Gibbs mass has four pieces: the ordinary cloud, each rare strip away from its intersection, and the crossed address. Each piece is diffuse in its remaining critical degrees of freedom. The finite windows isolate the two boundary-layer intensities. The echo numerator selects distinct states from the crossed address while its denominator includes all four pieces.

The oracle integrates the two finite rare-displacement windows directly. The independent reference changes from displacement to Boltzmann mass and does the inner integral analytically. It also checks the ordinary Gaussian cloud against a symmetric trivariate orthant formula. The shortcut keeps the ordinary cloud and the crossed address while omitting the two rare strips.
