# Author analysis

This original two-temperature-like spectroscopy problem is inspired by canonical thermal ensembles in the [Princeton physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

Let λ be amplification and (\alpha=1/\lambda\). A family's extreme displacement is \(z=X+Na v_F-\log N/(2c)\). The root spectrum on finite z converges to a Poisson process of intensity (G_F(0)e^{cz}dz), where (G_F,G_L) are the two scaled Gaussian densities. In setting 0 root weights are (W=e^{-\lambda c z}) after removing the common (N^{-\lambda/2}) factor. Each unconditioned root has an asymptotically deterministic critical leaf cloud of mass (L=\int_0^\infty G_L(y)e^{(c\,\mathrm{cold\_field}-\mathrm{cold\_window})y}dy). The omitted high-z root tail is bounded by its finite annealed leaf mean times an integral of (e^{-(\lambda-1)cz}\). The probability of a root far below the window also tends to zero when the truncation is widened.

Condition on a root at z and a site of that root at finite unshifted leaf displacement t. The scaled expected number of such sites per root is (\sqrt N\, d\mathbb E\mathrm{count}\to G_L(0)e^{ct}dt). In setting 0 the ordinary leaf cloud of this forced root remains, and this site adds (e^{-ct}\) to its cloud mass. Other roots contribute (L S\), with \(S=\sum W\) from the original root Poisson process by Palm independence. Thus the full conditional denominator is (L S+W(L+e^{-ct})\).

In setting 1 the ordinary Gibbs mass is (B\) from the tilted Gaussian on \(x\geq0,x+y\geq0\). The selected site has weight \(N^{-1/2}e^{-c(z+t)}\), and the selected family also contributes only order \(N^{-1/2}\). This gives \(\sqrt N\) times its Gibbs selection probability tending to \(e^{-c(z+t)}/B\). Counts on a fixed strictly positive rectangular subregion give an exponentially accurate lower bound for the ordinary mass, permitting convergence of the inverse normalizer here. Multiplying both rare factors and the root count cancels (e^{c(z+t)}\) over the compact recorded windows. The remaining result is (G_F(0)G_L(0)/B\) times the integral of the setting 0 tag probability over z and t.

The Laplace transform of (S\) is \(\exp[-K s^\alpha]\) with (K=G_F(0)\Gamma(1-\alpha)/c\). Write (a_0=W(L+e^{-ct})\). Use the reciprocal moment of (a_0+L S\) for the setting 0 draw, multiplying by (W e^{-ct}\). For an outside draw with cloud band mass (L_b\) multiply the integrand before averaging by (L_b S/(a_0+L S)\). Its positive Laplace form uses \(\alpha K L^\alpha s^\alpha e^{-a_0 s-K(L s)^\alpha}\) multiplied by (L_b/L\).

The oracle uses this positive form and fixed, rescaled quadrature. The reference uses separate inverse moments with an adaptive integration and an independent ordinary cone integral. The completed shortcut keeps the correct root weight process but omits the tagged site from its own family's cold Gibbs normalizer.
