# Commensurate critical domain echo

This original model was inspired by discrete spin ensembles and rare fluctuations in the [Princeton physics general exam archive](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

Exponential tilting of each binomial law places its mean at the commensurate rational fraction. The ordinary Gibbs cloud has Gaussian fluctuations truncated by feasibility of the family count and of the full path energy. In a boundary family at a fixed integer displacement, the second count has its own positive-half Gaussian cloud. The parent boundary intensity is a discrete binomial local-limit weight. The echo sums over the actually addressed levels, each with its finite spectral spacing and its own nonlinear Gibbs normalization.

The reference reverses the Gaussian integration order, evaluates the echo in the unshifted density convention, and checks the rare parent intensity against exact finite binomial probabilities. The shortcut lets each addressed level represent a full continuous interval of counting measure centered at that level.
