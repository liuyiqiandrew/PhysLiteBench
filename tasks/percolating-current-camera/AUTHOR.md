# Cluster multiplicities on a critical torus

Each distinct winding conductor receives an independent random sign, and the same signs determine the gate and the measured fringe. For a primitive direction (a,b), a geometric realization with n winding conductors has 2n winding interfaces. At critical bond probability the two orientations of each interface yield a weight 2 cos(πe/2). A weight z per conducting cluster thus requires 4 cos²(πe/2)=z. The contribution of nonzero Gaussian winding k carries cos(πke) with the zero-cluster term removed. The resulting polynomial is Tₖ(z/2−1)−(−1)ᵏ, with T the Chebyshev polynomial. For the public lattice convention the geometrical factor is exp[−2πk²|(a+b s)+i b r|²/(3r)]. At z=1 this agrees with the open winding-direction weight. The characteristic function of one independent tag supplies z for each Fourier component of the detector. The oracle sums those Fourier components with the gate filter before normalizing. [Arguin, Homology of Fortuin–Kasteleyn clusters on the torus](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0111193) describes the loop weights and homology partition functions. [Pruessner and Moloney](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0310361) give independent tests of the unmarked percolation law.

The private reference explicitly expands the polynomial into the probabilities for one, two, three and further distinct clusters. For each count it sums its binomial sign assignments against the integer clock and cosine readout. All 24 predictions agree with the oracle within 5e−10. The unmarked probabilities also agree with Pinson's three-Gaussian-sum form. A microscopic union-find simulation opens individual bonds at probability 1/2 and recovers cluster winding from lifted displacements. For each of four geometries, 96,000 independent 24-site-wide films give every held-out reading within 2.15 estimated standard errors of the continuum solution. The largest finite-sample discrepancy is 0.00715. Grading uses the independently reproduced continuum values.

The completed control uses the exact multiplicity law and both meters but assigns one polarity to all winding clusters in each film. The neutral, nonzero-residue and oblique maximum errors are 0.840, 0.116 and 0.557 against tolerance 0.004.
