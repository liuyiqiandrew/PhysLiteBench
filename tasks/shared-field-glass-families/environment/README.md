# Shared-field glass families

A frozen random energy landscape has floor(exp(N s_0)) families, each containing floor(exp(N s_1)) leaves. The arrays `entropies` and `variances` supply s and v in family, leaf order. Independently draw the family energy from a centered Gaussian of variance N gain² v_0, and each leaf energy increment from a centered Gaussian of variance N gain² v_1. Each family also has a fixed independent equiprobable mark m equal to +1 or −1. A leaf energy is its family energy plus its individual increment. At field h, subtract h m from the energy of every leaf in that family.

In an `overlap_record` all draws use the same landscape and the same `inverse_temperature`. There are two available fields in `fields`. For row i choose the field indexed by `settings[i]`, either 0 or 1, and draw one independent thermal leaf at that field. Each row of `record` has a family label and a within-family leaf label. Equality of the first entries requires the same family, and equality of both entries requires the same leaf. Different entries specify distinct branches at that depth. The actual numeric labels are arbitrary. A value +1 or −1 in `root_filter` additionally requires that row's family to have that fixed mark. Zero accepts either mark. Return the probability of the complete event, first forming Gibbs probabilities in the finite common landscape, then averaging over disorder and taking N to infinity.

The ratio s_0/v_0 is strictly smaller than s_1/v_1, and the overlap experiments are colder than both freezing temperatures. The separate `energy_density` calibration uses zero field at a specified high-temperature inverse_temperature and measures the mean Gibbs energy divided by N. Gain is in [0.60,1.36]. Implement `Model.fit(records)` and `Model.predict(experiments)` in `model.py`. Records contain `input`, `value` and `sigma`. Store the fit as `self.gain` and return one finite scalar per experiment in input order.
