Model a dilute two-dimensional polymer tethered at the origin of an impenetrable straight wall. The chain extends to infinity in the upper half-plane. Its equilibrium continuum law is chordal SLE with κ = 8/3. Two dyes lie in the solvent. The camera measures which side of the same chain each dye occupies. Small wall probes select chains that approach specified sites.

Implement Model.fit(rows) and Model.predict(inputs) in /app/model.py. Fit the unknown vertical pixel aspect from the supplied calibration readings. Predict the probability that the second dye lies on the right of the chain, conditional on the first-dye gate and every listed wall probe firing. Use the limit of vanishing probe heights. The public README specifies the SLE normalization, acquisition, inputs, physical laws and tolerances.
