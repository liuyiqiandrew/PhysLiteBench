A dilute polymer forms a planar loop tethered at the origin of a straight wall. The camera reports the fraction of loops enclosing a second dye, among those enclosing a first dye and touching every indicated passive wall probe. A radius sensor simultaneously selects a narrow band of maximum loop radius. Take the limit of zero band width and vanishing probe heights, as defined in the public README.

Fit the vertical camera aspect from the single-dye calibration. Implement Model.fit(rows) and Model.predict(inputs) in /app/model.py for the equilibrium SLE(8/3) bubble apparatus. The README supplies the continuum law, conventions, worked readings, inputs and grading tolerances.
