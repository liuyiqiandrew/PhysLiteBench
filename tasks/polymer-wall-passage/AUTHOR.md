# Polymer wall passage

The continuum model is defined to be chordal SLE(8/3). A passive contact is the event of intersecting a small vertical slit. For each slit at x the map fixing the origin has displacement a V(z) to first order in its capacity, with V(z)=1/(z−x)+1/x. The derivative at the origin gives the restriction weight. The displacement of the remaining wall coordinate y and the change in its local capacity (a squared derivative) both enter the second-order contact measurement. Applying the resulting operator to the constant, the two one-point marginals and the joint right-passage probability gives all pieces of the physical readout. Both fields are observed on the same chain.

The two-dye angular law comes from [Beliaev and Johansson Viklund, theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.5206). The avoidance and conditional map laws are given in [Lawler, Schramm and Werner, Conformal restriction](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0209343).

The oracle uses the hypergeometric angular law and centered derivatives of the contact recurrence. The separate reference integrates the angular ODE, removes finite positive slits with the exact square-root map, and extrapolates decreasing capacities in the opposite order. The validator checks both against the analytic two-contact density, wall and dye exchange, reflection, scale invariance, and the shared-noise backward equation. For the 30 hidden settings the maximum oracle discrepancy is below 5e−8. The smallest first-dye fraction after wall selection exceeds 0.015. The completed control has the exact contact-selected marginal of each dye but uses two independent chains for their conditional passage. Its maximum errors exceed 0.27 in every group. Calibration uses only the open camera with a single dye. The model and files fit within the repository's 1,800-second agent and 180-second grading limits used for this expansion.
