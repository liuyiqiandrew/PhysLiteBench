A camera measures the last toppling wave of an avalanche on a strip of long granular tracks. Select the wave by the measured deficit in the drive junction's final height. Different fabricated samples have different track lengths and bond multiplicities. Their fabrication probabilities apply before all dynamical and optical gates.

Fit the shutter rate and implement Model in /app/model.py. The public README specifies the recurrent ensemble, grain transfers, finite-size sampling order, clock conventions and reference readings.
