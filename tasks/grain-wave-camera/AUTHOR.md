# Granular wave camera

Stationary waves are in bijection with spanning forests rooted at the drive and the drain. The drive component is the wave. The synchronous time at a site is its depth in the rooted wave tree. In the burning construction, a site toppling in a given round can use an eligible incoming bond from the preceding round. The number of such choices is the number of local stable heights giving the same first toppling round. The wave is last when at least one microscopic neighbor fails to return a grain to the drive. [Ivashkevich, Ktitarev and Priezzhev](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-4371(94)90188-0) give the stationary wave correspondence.

Contract the long tracks to junction edges. Each omitted track contributes Nl choices for one missing bond. Given the coarse forest the cuts are independent, but positions on the same track see the same cut. The common power of N cancels. A missing track from the drive to a drain-component junction prevents a return only if its cut is the first microscopic bond. This occurs with probability 1/(Nl). In the final-wave limit distinct first-bond cuts add to leading order; the selected coarse forest and tagged port are consequently biased by inverse track length. All other missing bonds remain uniformly distributed. The pulse time for a site is the unique path distance from the drive on the retained side of its track. A common shutter weights the pair by its later flash.

The oracle enumerates edge subsets and computes the leading limiting weights. The independent finite-N reference enumerates oriented junction parents, resolves all possible cut positions into distinct clock categories, and tests the actual first neighbors. Extrapolation from 128 and 256 bonds per unit length agrees within 0.00000037 on every held-out prediction. Direct enumeration of all stable heights on two small microscopic subdivisions independently checks all three wave classes using grain transfers and actual synchronous clocks, without using any forest law. The completed shortcut knows the forest, the two subtrees, the propagation times and the cut at the terminal track, but counts eligible terminal tracks equally. Relative to the correct microscopic return selection, it matches every all-wave reading and misses the last and port groups by 0.202 and 0.0852. The tolerance is 0.004.
