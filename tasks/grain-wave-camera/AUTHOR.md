# Charge-resolved granular waves

Each stationary toppling wave is a spanning forest with components rooted at the drive and the drain. Depth in the drive tree is the synchronous toppling clock. Each eligible parent bond from the preceding round corresponds to a grain height giving that first toppling round, including the multiplicity of parallel contacts. [Ivashkevich, Ktitarev and Priezzhev](https://doi.org/10.1016/0378-4371(94)90188-0) describe the wave correspondence.

A retained track contributes one of its parallel channels at every microscopic step. An omitted track lacks one whole step, giving its cut a weight proportional to the reciprocal channel count at that step. Its macroscopic resistance is l(1/c+1/d)/2. Before size selection the expected number of waves emitted per added grain is the ratio of the two-root forest partition function to the ordinary spanning-tree partition function. The domain's ordinary tree count is therefore essential when pooling raw shots. Both counts may be reduced to macroscopic tracks of conductance equal to the reciprocal resistance.

At the start of the final wave the drive has exactly its threshold height. Its final deficit sums the bond multiplicities of its immediate microscopic neighbors that failed to topple. For an omitted drive track ending in the drain component, an end cut has probability 1/(N times its resistance times the adjacent channel count). A prescribed deficit may be produced by one cut in one domain and multiple cuts in another. Insert the optical gate and shutter in the unnormalized weights and then select the smallest cut count with nonzero accepted weight across all domains. The implementation identifies the leading order before rescaling the corresponding domain priors, including very small positive priors. A shared cut on an observed track governs both pickups on that track.

The oracle sums limiting cut weights on undirected forests. The independent calculation enumerates oriented junction parents, computes the ordinary tree partition separately, places all microscopic cuts in disjoint finite categories, and tests the actual missing grain count. Extrapolation from N=1024 and 2048 agrees within 0.00000056 across 21 readings. Two weighted microscopic graphs were independently checked by enumerating every stable height and physically burning, driving and toppling every recurrent state in parallel. At N=4 this enumeration gives 160 recurrent configurations and 976 waves.

The completed control knows each domain's exact dynamics, atom weights and macroscopic optical selection, but averages individually normalized domain readings with the fabrication weights. It misses the three groups by 0.092, 0.233 and 0.049, against tolerance 0.004.
