# Coincident terminal packets in an avalanche photodiode

Revision 2 resolves the last electron and the last hole packets on the same shot and records both their heights. Impact multiplication and shared-bias feedback are as in the single-contact experiment. The dark-flight calibration fixes a=0.92. Impact branching with dead space is treated by [Saleh, Hayat and Teich](https://people.bu.edu/teich/pdfs/IEEE-ED-37-1976-1990.pdf). The displacement current measured during drift follows the [Shockley–Ramo law](https://doi.org/10.1109/JRPROC.1939.228757).

A pair born at (t,x) produces an electron contact time E=t+(1−x)/vₑ and a hole time H=t+x/vₕ. An electron impact leaves E unchanged and starts a new H packet. A hole impact leaves H unchanged and starts a new E packet. Thus either terminal packet can have several co-moving carriers from one avalanche tree. The joint law has a one-clock atom when just one species ionizes and a two-dimensional density when both clocks are delayed. Multiplying two height distributions can assign probability to combinations excluded by the common ancestry.

## Calculations

Gain fixes the number of impacts. Enumerate their chronological parent labels and identify the event that initiated each last delayed packet. A positive electron delay pins a hole impact time, with Jacobian vₑ/(vₑ+vₕ); a positive hole delay pins an electron impact time, with Jacobian vₕ/(vₑ+vₕ). If a contact is prompt, exclude events that would start a new packet on it. Both terminal maxima and all dead-flight and collection constraints apply to the same birth times. Through gain five at most two times are free in the supported preparations. The solution clips their affine domain into polygons or intervals, partitions it at gate crossings, and accumulates a table of joint packet probabilities and waveform moments.

The likelihood contains the selected parent rates at impact and survival against every other impact through collection. Voltage depression uses the total displacement of all carriers. The solution differences a primitive of exp(−feedback×Q) over each carrier's eligible time segments, including the fresh dead flight after an impact. The independent reference reconstructs the genealogy numerically on affine laboratory-time coordinates, builds upper and lower event-time limits in horizontal strips, and counts energized carriers on every interval of the timeline. It evaluates its integrated hazard from these counts and directly sums each pair's two displacements. It never clips polygons or uses the solution's field-factor primitive. Each implementation accumulates tagged and untagged selected histories on its own.

In the public equal-rate, zero-dead-space, clamped-bias witness the integrated hazard is 2aG on every gain-G history because total displacement is G. Each of the three admissible parent assignments has the same product of impact rates and time Jacobians. Their joint weights are therefore equal even though their terminal packet heights and gate charges differ. Other checks include the unity of the total height distribution, recovery of the unclipped waveform after summing all packet heights, and direct unconditioned stochastic sampling. The public worked shot is separate from every held-out geometry.

The starter uses five representative positions per unpinned impact and neglects the probability of avoiding extra events. The completed negative control calculates the full dynamics, all tagged waveform readings and both exact terminal height marginals at the given gain and times. It multiplies the marginals for a joint height fraction. This preserves the two waveform groups and fails the coincidence group.
