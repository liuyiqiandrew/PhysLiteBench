## Time selection

Label the primary pair 0 and each impact-created pair j in chronological order. Gain G fixes G−1 impacts. The incident carrier can keep its label when its dead clock resets. An electron impact creates another electron on exactly the same drift trajectory, so both contribute to one terminal packet. A hole impact starts a new electron packet. If its parent hole was born at time b and position x, an impact at t sends an electron to the contact at t+(1−x+vₕ(t−b))/vₑ. Fixing that packet's arrival fixes t and contributes density factor vₑ/(vₑ+vₕ). Each delayed history has a unique last packet origin almost surely, even when multiple electrons in that packet arrive together. Enforce its observed multiplicity and the requirement that every other packet arrive earlier. For the prompt atom every event must have an electron parent. Include its full probability mass.

For a specified history, the density is the product of the selected parent hazards at their impact times times exp(−∫λ_total dt). The integral spans the entire history through collection, enforcing the exact gain and excluding additional impacts. Resetting a parent removes its hazard throughout its new dead flight. A tag multiplies this law by its acceptance probability before conditioning.

## Independent calculations

The solution represents the free chronological event times, all birth positions, collection times and dead-flight endpoints as affine functions. The legal domain is a point, interval or convex polygon. It clips the domain by physical inequalities, divides it at gate crossings, and integrates smooth subregions with a Gauss rule. The path likelihood uses a primitive of exp(−feedback×Q) and differences of this primitive over each eligible carrier segment. Integrals use the stable divided difference of the exponential. There are at most two free times in any supported shot.

The independent reference grows labelled histories forward from their available flight intervals. Each free event time is parametrized by a fraction of its parent's remaining eligible flight. A pinned last-packet event uses the density Jacobian above. It reconstructs the total current directly from each new pair and integrates the shared hazard by counting the active, eligible carriers between sorted trajectory breakpoints.
