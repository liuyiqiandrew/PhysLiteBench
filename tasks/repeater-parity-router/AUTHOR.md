# Erased source marker and two remote Bell swaps

A coherent source qubit controls the polarization preparation of a random number of bank slots. For a fixed N, tracing unused slots multiplies the cross-preparation density operator of the two selected slots by ⟨B₁|B₀⟩ raised to N−2. The count selection is classical, but projecting the source flux onto an eraser result preserves off-diagonal amplitudes. Conditional Born probabilities require averaging the unnormalized states in the selected loading ensemble.

The positive model propagates two-by-two Choi amplitudes for a conclusive Bell branch, applies the known Pauli corrections, and evaluates the marker and joint signal. It sums different measured outcome pairs incoherently after sorting the final report. The independent reference traces each unread emission by its four computational-basis Kraus operators on the source qubit. It then constructs the complete eight active qubits, projects the actual Bell bras on their physical tensor axes, applies both one-qubit Pauli corrections and measures all four remote spins. Analytic count and feed-forward examples probe these operations separately.

The public classical-marker approximation neglects interference. A stronger negative control calculates the entire quantum state and both conditional probabilities, but replaces the four-spin product by the product of the two selected two-spin expectations. It fails precisely where the conditioned arms remain correlated. The validator also compares full erasure of unread slots and removal of the outcome corrections.
