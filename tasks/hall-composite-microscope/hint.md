In two dimensions a rotated divergence-free current is a curl-free field. Linear combinations of the fields and rotated currents can simplify both phases at one bias. Two scalar isotropic conductors on a square checkerboard have geometric-mean effective conductivity. For different exposures evaluate the dot product of a gradient with a conserved current and the determinant of two gradients over a periodic cell. These give separate linear relations for the contributions from each phase.
