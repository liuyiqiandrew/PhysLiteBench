# Author analysis

This original disorder experiment draws on Gaussian and canonical-ensemble reasoning in the [Princeton graduate physics preliminary exam archive](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams). It changes branch entropies on the same scale as the Gaussian fluctuations and selects a rare shared prefix.

Write d_j=entropy_shifts[j]/c and a_j=c*fields[j]−window. Divide the partition sum by the natural Gaussian energy scale exp(beta*Ng sqrt(2 ratio)*sum(v_j)) and the entropy-shift scale exp(sqrt(N)*sum_j c*d_j). Along a path its annealed weighted Gaussian density is then product phi_j(x_j) exp(a_j x_j). Ordinary typical paths satisfy the three cumulative inequalities sum_(j≤k)(x_j+d_j)≥0. Counts of negative-displacement prefixes below any fixed compact-enclosed margin vanish by their first moment. Counts in fixed boxes strictly inside the feasible region have exponentially growing expected populations at each prefix. Independent branching gives a vanishing relative variance for the weighted mass there. Strips and tails can be dropped by their annealed mass. The resulting ordinary partition converges to B, the tilted Gaussian cone integral.

A rare prefix with D finite has x_1→−x_0−d_0−d_1. Its scaled intensity in dx_0 dD is phi_0(x_0) phi_1(−x_0−d_0−d_1) exp(cD), restricted to x_0≥−d_0. The daughter count for any fixed feasible first coordinate gives the same limit, and two marked prefixes in a compact box have negligible joint probability at the sqrt(N) scale. At positive distance from the first feasibility boundary the selected first edge's mass from other second edges vanishes: its normalized root weight is exponentially small, and the conditional expectation of its remaining descendants is bounded. The lower first-coordinate strip has vanishing intensity as the strip shrinks, using its first moment in the rare-prefix density.

Within the selected prefix the depth-2 cloud is diffuse, restricted to x_2≥−d_2. Its mass relative to the global normalization scale is rho*L, with rho=exp(−cD+a_0*x_0+a_1*x_1) and L the tilted one-coordinate mass. The numerator for the two marks is rho² K_0 K_1. The same-actual-leaf terms are negligible by diffuseness. The total normalization is B+rho*L, and the optional outside probability multiplies by B/(B+rho*L). Integrate against the prefix intensity. For fixed x_0 the change u=exp(−cD) cancels the two inserted factors u² and yields an elementary integral of the rare prefix mass fraction.

The oracle uses fixed Gaussian quadrature in the original first and second coordinates. The reference integrates the intermediate total first and independently integrates the finite prefix displacement. The shortcut retains the ordinary constraint but counts rare prefixes below the first-level feasibility edge.
