# Author analysis

This original thermal readout is inspired by canonical weights and shared degrees of freedom in the [Princeton physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams).

Each setting's ordinary partition is the critical tilted Gaussian integral on the cone x≥0 and x+y≥0 for its own color. Opposite-color sites multiply that mass by exp(−c*offset) if the leakage is zero. A positive leakage suppresses the whole opposite bank in probability after normalization. Compact interior strips concentrate by counts and the shared family covariance. The expected count outside any negatively separated prefix tends to zero. This also controls the two ordinary masses jointly since only two settings are used.

Use one ordered red and blue pair in the same family. Conditional on X=√N*u with bounded u, the two finite line densities have joint prefactor 1/(2πN gain² v_L). Their leading exponential cancels the pair count and exp(N K). The opposite linear terms in u cancel, leaving exp(κ*(t0+t1)−u²/(gain² v_L)), where κ=c(v_F+v_L)/v_L. In the balanced case X is finite and the last Gaussian factor tends to one. The extra N^1/2 in that case removes the small finite root interval density. A chosen family here has its signed root near zero on the N scale. All of its other sites make a vanishing contribution in conditional expectation. The remaining families retain their original limiting ordinary masses.

With X=√N*u, wrong-color forced sites have extra displacement proportional to leakage[0]+2u and leakage[1]−2u respectively. If either is negative, one denominator diverges and the target joint probability tends to zero. In the open slab both crossed terms vanish, leaving one finite tagged mass in each denominator. The slab boundaries have measure zero. With finite X and zero leakages, each forced site's weight is finite in both settings, so both must enter both denominators. An outside draw contributes the ordinary mass divided by its selected denominator. Bounded conditional probabilities on the compact displacement boxes give dominated convergence after multiplying the exact mean pair measure by the stated scale.

The oracle integrates the ordinary cone conditional on the total fluctuation and uses forced Gibbs weights as integration variables in the balanced case. The reference integrates ordinary mass conditional on the first coordinate and keeps the three displacement variables in the pair law. The completed shortcut adds only each color's direct selected line to its own denominator in the balanced case.
