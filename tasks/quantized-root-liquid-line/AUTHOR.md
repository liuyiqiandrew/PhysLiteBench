Multiplying the tilted count densities by their exponential critical factors gives the Gaussian fluctuation limit on x≥0 and x+y≥0. The strict-margin count and shared-family covariance bounds make the normalized cloud concentrate. The corresponding single-family mass has y≥0.

At root index k the raw physical displacement is t=gain*(k−residue/3). Its scaled count is gain*phi_F(0)*exp(ct), from the tilted binomial local limit at lattice span gain. Conditional on that root, a site at its own finite increment d has scaled density phi_L(0)*exp(cd)dd from the tilted Gamma density. The two count scales give N. Conditioning one actual site leaves both the ordinary tree and the selected family's other liquid configurations in their original laws.

Each denominator therefore contains an ordinary mass, exp(−ct) times the forced family's diffuse cloud, and the finite selected mass exp(−c(t+d)). A sibling draw only uses the marked diffuse cloud and an outside draw uses the marked ordinary cone. The oracle changes variables to the leaf Gibbs weight and the reference uses d. The completed shortcut omits the selected atom from both denominators while retaining the quantized root and the diffuse selected family.
