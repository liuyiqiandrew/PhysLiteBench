# Two phase sites in selected families

This original constrained-equilibrium problem uses frozen phases, relative weights, and thermal observations from [Princeton's graduate physics preliminary exams](https://phy.princeton.edu/academics/graduate-program/degree-requirements/preliminary-exams). Each family has its own pair of competing site phases with different freezing scales.

The two independent site processes contribute unnormalized stable Gibbs masses with two different exponents. Thermal family selection biases the joint child process by the sum of those two masses raised to the root index. This bias changes the observed phase fractions and the within-phase coincidences. The completed approximation keeps the two jump scales and joint normalization and omits the family bias.

The oracle normalizes by the fractional moment of the total two-phase mass. The independent reference uses an integration-by-parts formula for that moment and a labelled factorial expansion. A direct simulation of tilted site Poisson processes and a single-exponent limit check the thermal and uniform protocols.
