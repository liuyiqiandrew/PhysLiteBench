# Fission coincidence camera

The response is the joint color-count distribution of the entire recoiling family until extinction. Source preparation is independent of later fission. The extinction rate of the corresponding dominating birth–death process is positive because each carrier splits at rate at most 0.36 and jumps out of the live interval at rate at least 2D/π ≥ 0.477.

For any two-color counting weight let U(x) = 1 − E[r^R b^B] for the eventual family from x. Given a split at z, the family hit weight becomes U(z−d)+U(z+d)−U(z−d)U(z+d). The one-interval killed Cauchy operator multiplied by D, plus the fission rate λ, applied to U gives the jump source from terminal landings and λ times this split weight. The outside boundary values include immediate daughter absorption at placement.

The production solver uses six real channels. Three track at least one red, one blue or either color. Three track exactly one red only, one blue only or one of each. Independence of the two subfamilies is conditional on their actual birth points. Weighted Chebyshev modes represent the single-island generator; the angular quadrature splits at recoil-shifted channel and counter edges. [Fractional Laplace operator and Meijer G-function](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.08529)

The independent reference solves the nonlinear first-exit equation with the interval's logarithmic Green kernel, explicit exit tail masses and angular piecewise linear interpolation. It evaluates the two-variable probability generating function on a complex torus, using a discrete Fourier coefficient to recover the exact-one-of-each probability. The coefficient alias error is bounded by 0.12⁶ before conditioning. This method uses neither the Chebyshev representation nor the six-channel recursion. [Spectral properties of the Cauchy process on half-line and interval](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/pdq010)

The 27 held-out labels use the independent 138-node Green calculation. On these rows the 88-mode generator differs by at most 0.000037, compared to tolerance 0.004. A pre-freeze test at 64 combinations of operating extrema found maximum changes below 0.000244 from 45 to 88 modes. Independent Green solutions on 11 of those settings agreed to within 0.000054. The public fission-disabled pair has a direct single-particle Poisson-kernel calculation and every coincidence has exactly two clicks. The public fission-enabled shot was computed independently by both solvers.

The upper tail of family multiplicity is observable. The one-generation starter misses the conditional clean fraction by 0.274. A more selective control retains all generations and the physical initial pair, but sends both daughters in the same random direction at each later fission. That control preserves the displacement distribution of a randomly tagged daughter, yet misses red probability by 0.0056, conditional coincidence by 0.0076 and the clean fraction by 0.0326.
