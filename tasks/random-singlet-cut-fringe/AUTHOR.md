# Random-singlet cut fringe, two-cut revision

A strongly disordered antiferromagnetic XX chain has singlets across a hierarchy of exchange energies. This experiment selects two cuts with one or two active spins between them. At lower energies first the original spins disappear and eventually a common effective bond supplies singlets across both cuts. The local flow in the public instrument defines the quantitative fixed-point model. The Ramsey and matched-copy herald are idealized energy-resolving operations. For the single-cut background and renewal law, see [Fagotti, Calabrese and Moore, Phys. Rev. B 83, 045110 (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.83.045110).

## Reduction of two boundary links

Write Eₖ(x)=k exp(−kx), and let ∗ denote convolution on the nonnegative half-line. The inner link between the two original central spins has only deterministic drift while both remain. Its conditional density is Eₖ with k=exp(μ) and its loss flux is k. A boundary with one exterior neighbor and no inner decimations has forward generator L₁f=∂ₓ[(1+x)f]−f+E₁∗f. A boundary born as E₁ retains that shape and is lost at rate one.

If an outer bond decimates before the inner bond, its new boundary joins the still-present inner link to an exterior one. Its density is Cₖ=E₁∗Eₖ. Subtracting the explicit derivative k∂ₖCₖ from L₁Cₖ gives k(E₁−Cₖ). The other boundary is still E₁. If that other boundary then decimates before the phase Cₖ activates, the common bond has density Dₖ=E₁∗E₁∗Eₖ. With two exterior neighbors its generator is L₂f=∂ₓ[(1+x)f]−2f+2E₁∗f. It obeys L₂Dₖ−k∂ₖDₖ=k(E₁∗E₁−Dₖ). Thus this common bond has a pending interior degree of freedom. Only after its activation does it have the ordinary distribution immediately after a crossing. All three identities follow directly by Laplace transforming in x.

For an ordinary common bond, the density before the next crossing stays (a+b x)exp(−x), with a′=−2a+b and b′=a−b. At a crossing it restarts with a=0,b=1. The resulting interarrival time is a sum of independent exponentials with rates (3−√5)/2 and (3+√5)/2. A left-only or joint crossing reverses the sign of the exterior left endpoint. A right-only crossing leaves it unchanged. With m centers the left endpoint of a right-only pair has sign (−1)ᵐ times the current exterior left sign.

## Numerical checks

The oracle evolves the two-cut phase populations continuously. Its generator includes the nonstationary inner rate exp(μ) and the two ordinary renewal stages. The reference integrates separately over the actual starting interior exchange x, for which the activation coordinate is ln(1+x). Given this coordinate, it constructs constant pre- and post-activation propagators on each band, using the marked-bond density coefficients for the common cut. It performs deterministic quadrature over the activation coordinate and keeps separate spin and count traces. It computes spin matrix elements with a separate singlet representation. Neither code samples a finite chain.

The worked one-sided scan has no loss of coherence when the interior bond decimates first. Its survival with two central spins is 1−∫₀¹2 exp[−2u+1−exp(u)] du. With one central spin it survives only if both boundary clocks survive through the band. The validator checks both formulas and invariance under splitting a band. The continuously resolved and fixed-interior-exchange solutions agree on all 24 unseen measurements.

The starter assigns three independent stationary streams to the three crossing geometries. A second control keeps the correct one-center and ordinary common-bond physics, but regenerates the common link immediately after two outward decimations. This agrees on every one-center scan and changes both of the two-center groups. The heralded group preferentially selects those two one-sided events. There are 12 empty-scan calibrations. The solve and verifier allowances are 1,800 and 180 seconds.
