Each mobile internal level transports its own state energy. Let p_i(T)=g_i*exp(-E_i/T)/[1+sum_j g_j*exp(-E_j/T)], R=sum_i r_i*p_i, A=sum_i E_i*r_i*p_i and U=sum_i E_i*p_i, with r=(1,0.4). The total number flux is j=-D*grad(R*c), and total energy density is C*T+U*c. The particle contribution to energy flux is -D*grad(A*c), not just (A/R)*j. Relative transport among internal levels supplies an extra thermal conduction term even at zero net particle flux. Use that full energy flux with the same fitted D.
