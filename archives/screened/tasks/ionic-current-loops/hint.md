Local electroneutrality requires divergence-free charge current, not zero charge current at every point. Let g=sum_i z_i D_i c_i and kappa=sum_i D_i c_i. The periodic electrostatic potential satisfies div(kappa*grad(phi))=-laplacian(g). The vector -grad(g)/kappa generally has nonzero curl in two dimensions and cannot be used as grad(phi). Solve the scalar potential equation, form each species flux, and project its divergence onto the measured Fourier mode. Divergence-free internal current loops need no electrodes.
