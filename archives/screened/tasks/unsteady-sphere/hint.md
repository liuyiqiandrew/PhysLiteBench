Small convective Reynolds number does not remove linear fluid inertia. The steady Stokes drag cannot simply be inserted into the particle's finite-frequency Newton equation. Solving the exterior unsteady Stokes flow gives fluid impedance 6*pi*eta*a*(1+a*k+(a*k)^2/9), where k=sqrt(-i*omega*rho/eta) with positive real part for the stated exp(-i*omega*t) convention. The square-root term is viscous history; the quadratic term gives half the displaced fluid mass. Include the particle inertia separately and retain the stated sine/cosine convention.
