Static elimination of the second coordinate gives the correct mean force response. It does not generally make the reduced quantum probe a Gibbs state of that static effective oscillator. The prescribed canonical state belongs to the whole interacting pair; obtain probe fluctuations by tracing that state over the other oscillator. For this quadratic Hamiltonian, both normal modes contribute quantum thermal fluctuations, with different frequencies and probe participation factors. Check the zero-coupling and high-temperature limits.
