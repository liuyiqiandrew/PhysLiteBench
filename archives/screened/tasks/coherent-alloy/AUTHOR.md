# Coherent alloy diffusion, revision 1

Fixed mean strain does not impose pointwise clamping on a coherent periodic solid. The supplied predictor uses the complete local clamped elastic energy and solves the corresponding conserved gradient diffusion correctly. Calibration wavevectors lie along x, and the prescribed traceless eigenstrain has zero x eigenstress. Hence no elastic force is generated and zero displacement is exactly mechanical equilibrium; the clamped approximation fits every calibration and identifies mobility.

A general concentration Fourier mode drives displacement through f=sigma0*n. Mechanical equilibration lowers its quadratic elastic penalty from B0=E0:C:E0=3 to B(n)=B0-f^T Gamma(n)^-1 f, where Gamma=3I+5nn^T. Along x this subtraction vanishes. For n=(0,1,1)/sqrt2 the stress-free deformation is a compatible shear and the entire elastic penalty vanishes; along y B=1.875. The chemical and gradient energies keep diffusion stable even for the completely relaxed elastic mode.

The oracle eliminates the three displacement components with the Christoffel matrix. The independent reference instead minimizes six strain components subject to the three transverse-transverse compatibility constraints, using a nine-dimensional saddle system. Its chemical penalty is twice the minimized elastic energy. Validation checks every342 allowed nonzero integer wavevector: compatibility, force balance, nonnegative penalty, independent prediction agreement and the analytic axis/shear limits.
