Revision2 supplies a complete energy- and momentum-conserving collision approximation. It relaxes branch energy to a common temperature and retains each branch drift separately. The physical normal collisions can exchange momentum between branches and retain only one shared drift.

For temperature-like angular energy perturbations f_i, branch energy is C_i<M_i0>, crystal momentum is C_i<M_i1>/v_i and heat flux is C_i*v_i<M_i1>. A common displaced Bose distribution gives normal target Tbar+3*mu*P/(v_i*W), where Tbar=sum(C_i*M_i0)/sum(C_i), P=sum(C_i*M_i1/v_i), W=sum(C_i/v_i²). A common normal rate makes this target conserve the instantaneous total moments exactly. The shortcut instead uses Tbar+3*mu*M_i1. Both controls conserve total energy and crystal momentum and dissipate the quadratic entropy norm, but the shortcut has an extra conserved branch-relative drift. This is an interbranch relaxation assumption, not a coefficient normalization error.

The known heat capacities follow the stated acoustic degeneracies and velocities, C_i=d_i/v_i³.
