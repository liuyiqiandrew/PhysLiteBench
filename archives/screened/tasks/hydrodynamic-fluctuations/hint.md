The liquid couples both dissipation and thermal fluctuations. For a constant mobility matrix M in one equilibrium bath, the velocity-increment covariance is 2 k_B T M dt. Applying deterministic Oseen coupling while assigning independent bead velocity noises does not satisfy fluctuation-dissipation. Propagate the covariance from its zero initial value using the full diffusion matrix; at late times it must approach the harmonic Gibbs covariance k_B T K^{-1}.
