# Film transmission, revision 1

The starter's coherent field transfer matrix and every dielectric boundary condition are correct. Its detector conversion assumes the entrance and exit wave admittances are equal, so it reports |t_E|² as transmittance. That assumption is exact for every calibration experiment, and the single thickness is identifiable over the complete stated interval. Changing the exit medium requires the ratio of normal Poynting fluxes: T=(n_R cos(theta_R))/(n_L cos(theta_L))*|t_E|². All indices and angles already affect the starter's amplitude calculation; the failure is the physical relation between field amplitude and transported energy.

The oracle multiplies by the TE admittance ratio. The independent reference solves four E/H interface equations for reflected, forward-film, backward-film, and transmitted fields; it uses no transfer-matrix formula. The detector planes are explicitly parallel to the interfaces, removing beam-normal intensity ambiguity. Checks cover power conservation, field continuity, the zero-thickness Fresnel limit, and a full-interval calibration likelihood sweep. Calibration and hidden power signals stay finite and between 0 and 1 for both controls; the wrong shortcut need not produce conspicuous T>1.

Hidden RMS errors are normalized by RMS reference transmittance, limit .04. The data fit and prediction margins are checked over 256 independent noise draws plus fitted-parameter extrema. Validation command: `uv run --python 3.13 --with numpy==2.3.3 --with scipy==1.16.3 --with pytest==8.4.2 python scripts/validate_film_transmission.py --noise-trials 256`. Generation is explicit with `--generate`. Concept approved by physics_review; final source review and evaluation pending.
