The calibration already contains the kinetic heat contribution for equal drag. Extending its coefficient by replacing unequal drag with a mean drag does not preserve the fast velocity moments that transport heat. Compute the stationary finite-mass heat balance before sending the mass to zero. For a temperature gradient along x, both the vx^3 moment and the mixed vx*vy^2 moment carry kinetic energy. Their dissipative relaxation rates follow separately from the two microscopic velocity equations; retain those rates instead of isotropizing the drag tensor. The leading local Gaussian second and fourth moments determine the sources of these third moments. Uniform temperature and equal drag are separate useful limits.
