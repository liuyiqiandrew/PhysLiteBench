# Quantum resonator bath revision

The public instruction is the unchanged neutral instruction used throughout this screen. The previous neutral task passed 2/3 Luna high trials; its complete sources and reviews are preserved in `archives/qubit-control-neutral-v1`.

This revision specifies the full probe–oscillator Hamiltonian and a factorized initial thermal bath. The starter includes the exact shared Gaussian phase covariance. It omits the coherent phase generated by the quantum bath commutator. For eigenvalues s and s' of the weighted probe operator, the reduced coherence multiplier is

    exp[-gamma (s-s')^2 sum_k a_k coth(omega_k/(2T))
         (1-cos(omega_k t))/omega_k^2]
    * exp[i gamma (s^2-s'^2) sum_k a_k
          (omega_k t-sin(omega_k t))/omega_k^2].

At T=0 the coth factor is one. With only one probe coupled, s squared is constant and the second factor is exactly one. Calibration therefore identifies gamma without distinguishing the two closures. With both probes coupled, the second factor produces a coherent probe interaction. The starter already includes all correlations of the shared classical Gaussian phase.

The private reference constructs each conditional oscillator Hamiltonian in a finite Fock basis and traces its unitary evolution against the thermal density matrix. It does not use the analytic reduced-coherence formula. Measurement probabilities are independently constructed with Bloch rotations. Cutoff checks from 40 to 56 and the peer check from 56 to 72 agree to about 1e-13.
