Track both number and energy locally. A mobile hop transports the excitation energy Delta along with its particle. With p(T)=1/(1+exp(Delta/T)), c_t=D*(p*c)_xx and the total energy density is C*T+Delta*p*c. Its flux contains Delta times the mobile number flux, in addition to the lattice Fourier flux. Thus [C+Delta*c*p'(T)]*T_t=kappa*T_xx+Delta*(1-p)*c_t in an isolated ring. Keep the same fitted D for calibration and prediction.
