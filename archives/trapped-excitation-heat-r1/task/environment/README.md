# Excitation transport in a solid ring

A fixed solid lattice forms a periodic ring of length 2*pi. Dilute, noninteracting particles each occupy either a bound internal state of energy zero or a mobile internal state of energy Delta=2. Boltzmann's constant and the lattice spacing used in the continuum coefficients are one. Both states have degeneracy one. There is no site exclusion or mechanical work. The particles have no additional kinetic or internal energy in this model.

Bound particles cannot move. A mobile particle hops symmetrically to neighboring lattice positions, with a temperature-independent hopping coefficient D. Each hop preserves its internal state and energy, and exchanges no heat with the lattice. In the continuum limit the mobile number flux is -D times the spatial gradient of mobile concentration. The shared unknown D is in [0.5,3].

At each position, transitions between bound and mobile states exchange their energy difference with the local lattice. These transitions obey detailed balance at the local temperature and are arbitrarily fast compared with spatial transport, so the internal populations are always in local equilibrium. The total concentration c is conserved. The lattice has constant heat capacity per length C=0.15, lattice energy density C*T, and Fourier thermal conductivity kappa=0.08. These coefficients do not include the particles. Ignore fluctuations, interactions, radiation, and heat exchange with the surroundings unless a temperature clamp is specified.

All quantities use the reduced units above. Initially c(x)=1+amplitude*cos(mode*x) and T(x)=temperature+thermal_amplitude*cos(mode*x), including equilibrium internal populations. The fields are periodic. The integer mode is 1, 2, or 3; amplitude is in [-0.35,0.35], temperature in [0.8,1.3], thermal_amplitude in [-0.08,0.08], and time in [0,8]. These initial conditions have positive concentration and temperature.

For clamped=true, an external thermostat holds T uniform at the given temperature for the whole measurement; thermal_amplitude is zero. For clamped=false the ring is isolated. The observable is either concentration or temperature. Return its signed cosine coefficient at wave number harmonic*mode, defined as (1/pi) integral from 0 to 2*pi of the field times cos(harmonic*mode*x). harmonic is 1 or 2. Constant spatial means do not contribute.

Calibration records use a uniform temperature clamp and measure the first concentration harmonic. Each contains input, value, and independent Gaussian standard deviation sigma. Implement Model.fit(records), returning self and setting Model.diffusivity, and Model.predict(experiments), returning one finite NumPy value per input. Fit the one diffusivity from data/calibration.json. You may revise the predictor to cover all allowed preparations.
