Charge transports entropy as well as producing Joule heat. Reciprocity gives
the local Peltier coefficient Pi=T*S(T), so the heat flux is Pi*J-k*dT/dx.
Combine its divergence with electrical work J*E in the local energy balance.
When S varies with temperature a bulk reversible heating term remains; using
S only in the terminal voltage misses this term. End reservoirs absorb the
contact heat while holding the specified temperatures.
