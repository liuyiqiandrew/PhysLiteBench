# Three physics outlines

These are unevaluated outlines. No task package or model evaluation was created. The companion audit hashes the actual archive material inspected; search counts alone are not used to claim novelty.

## 1. Chemical-route regeneration power — preferred

A single enzyme has three conformations connected in a ring. Each edge can react through either of two independently chemostatted fuel pairs. The common bath temperature, state free energies, channel stoichiometry, fuel chemical-potential drops and positive attempt rates are known. All elementary transition rates are specified and obey local detailed balance. One unknown positive scale multiplies every rate. Prepare the joint stationary process. Measure the reversible external power required to restore the consumed fuel reservoirs to their stated compositions, per enzyme. This is chemical free-energy draw, not a bare calorimetric heat signal.

The supplied approximation sums the parallel rates, solves the exact conformation generator, and gives each aggregate edge its log forward/backward rate ratio. It is the complete thermodynamics of a valid effective single-reservoir model and has positive entropy production. Every conformation statistic is exact. Equal channel affinities on every edge give an exact, nonzero calibration, with both channels active and energies/barriers varied. Power is linear in the common scale, so one nonzero measurement globally identifies it; multiple controls make calibration rich without relying on an approximate inverse problem.

Unequal chemical drops distinguish the reservoirs while leaving the aggregate dynamics perfectly Markovian. In a uniform ring with channel forward/backward rates (4,1) and (2,1), both net route currents point forward: physical power is 3 log(4)+log(2), while the aggregate model gives 4 log(3), a 9.43% discrepancy without a small signal. A stronger opposed-route illustration is preserved in assessment.json. A channel-marked counting generator gives an independent work reference; a cycle decomposition checks bookkeeping and positivity. Quantitative bounded-domain margins remain a prototype requirement.

The nearest archive is Floquet thermalization revision2: it also preserves dynamics while losing energy labels of physical transition channels. Its approximation assigns a common quasienergy to sideband jumps. Here the source instead computes the exact coarse Markov entropy, and the missing dissipation is a parallel chemical cycle. This is a different apparatus and closure, but a related physical-accounting theme; it is not claimed to be unrelated. Reactive piston, membrane partition, Soret–Dufour, structured mechanical baths and terminal-current noise were also read and differ in mechanism.

## 2. Single-copy isolated work extraction — reserve outline

Prepare independent copies of a stated three-level density matrix. Each experiment permits an arbitrary cyclic Hamiltonian protocol on that single system, starting and ending at H, with an ideal work source but no heat bath, measurements or ancillary systems. The readout is the maximum mean energy delivered to the source. Repetitions cannot be operated on jointly. A common positive scale of H is unknown.

A coherent macroscopic approximation computes the exact initial energy and entropy, finds the Gibbs state at the same entropy, and predicts the energy decrease to that state. The isolated finite system is instead confined to a unitary orbit. Calibration uses varied pure states, for which both optimizations reach the ground state and the scale enters linearly. The explicit diagonal example in assessment.json has a nonzero gap above4%. An independent unitary-orbit optimization or rearrangement bound can verify it. This is not the archived two-energy-record work cumulant problem, but pure-only calibration and the clearly isolated preparation may make it easy. It is not selected ahead of chemical routes.

## 3. Closed reaction-number noise — not recommended

Exactly N ideal molecules interconvert A and B in a heat bath; no particles enter or leave. A known applied level shift varies a single unknown intrinsic free-energy difference. A complete grand-canonical calculation chooses a fugacity to reproduce the fixed total mean and predicts independent Poisson populations. Every species mean matches the exact canonical binomial population, across N, temperature and applied shift; the unknown offset is globally identified by strict monotonicity. The hidden equilibrium variance is Np(1−p), while the source gives Np. For p between.25 and.75 the discrepancy is comfortably above4%. Fixed-N birth–death dynamics or the finite canonical partition function is an independent reference. The conservation issue is conspicuous and the archive already contains exclusion-current/competitive-adsorption tasks, so this is a lower-value fallback rather than a proposed build.
