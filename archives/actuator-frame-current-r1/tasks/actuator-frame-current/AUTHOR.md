The source retains their local covariance, the correct simultaneous inertial/correlation-time crossover, amplitude-induced drift, diffusion, and stationary x distribution. It replaces the physical force columns by the symmetric square root of their local covariance. This is a complete positive local-covariance model, but changing the force basis with position changes how the tracer samples each persistent channel.

Let a=1+contrast*cos(x), theta=winding*x+twist*sin(x), alpha=ratio and c=alpha/(1+alpha). At frozen x use u=sqrt(m)*v and z=sqrt(m)*eta. Their fast Ornstein-Uhlenbeck generator has drift (-u+Bz,-z/alpha), noise covariance 2D/alpha² in z, and corrector Phi=u+alpha*Bz satisfying -L0 Phi=u. The stationary cross covariance is <u z^T>=D*B/(1+alpha). Averaging the position derivative of the corrector gives the Ito position drift

    b_j = F_j + c*D*sum_k B_xk * partial_x B_jk.

The diffusion matrix is D*B*B^T=D*a²*I. For the stated rotation, the induced drift is c*D*(a*a',a²*theta'). The source uses half the spatial derivative of B*B^T, retaining the first component but losing the transverse term. Equal-position force statistics therefore cannot determine the physical circulation. This is an external nonequilibrium actuator, not an equilibrium thermal bath with a missing fluctuation-dissipation term.
