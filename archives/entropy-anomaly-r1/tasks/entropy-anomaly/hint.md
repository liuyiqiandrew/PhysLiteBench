Velocity elimination can preserve stationary positions and current without preserving heat exchanged with a spatially varying bath. Evaluate the entropy flux of the finite-mass Kramers dynamics before taking the small-mass limit; the discarded fast velocity fluctuations carry heat between temperatures. At stationarity the system entropy has zero mean rate. The velocity-moment balance relates the bath entropy to a temperature-weighted third velocity moment, whose leading behavior can be found using the local Gaussian second and fourth moments. The positional probability-current contribution alone does not include this kinetic heat transport. Check a zero-force temperature gradient and a uniform-temperature driven bath as separate limits.
