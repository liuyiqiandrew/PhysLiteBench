## Apparatus and energy balance

The fluid and units are those of the earlier slab, with gamma=.06, nu=.04 and unknown plasma frequency p in [.85,1.15]. Normal incidence removes any spatial phase averaging issue at the planar calorimeter. The new layer is massless and optically inert. It has known alpha=kappa/rho and tau=kappa/k_s, where rho=m*n0. Fluid slip is the sum of elastic-extension rate and dashpot velocity. The common traction obeys

```
T = k_s*e = kappa*v_d
v = e_dot + v_d
tau*T_dot + T = kappa*v.
```

The substrate remains fixed. Positive T is force transmitted by the fluid into the contact. Its opposite acts on the fluid. For current j=q*n0*v and scaled traction a=q*T/m, the linear boundary law is tau*a_dot+a=alpha*j. With exp(-i omega t), define Z=alpha/(1-i omega tau). The front and rear boundary conditions are nu*J_z=Z*J and nu*J_z=-Z*J, respectively. Tangential Maxwell fields are continuous, with one incoming wave at the front.

The full field solver includes both electromagnetic and viscous modes. It solves

```
E_zz + omega²*E = -i*omega*J
nu*J_zz = (gamma-i*omega)*J - p²*E.
```

The completed source calls the exact power T*v entering the layer calorimetric heat. The thermostat instead collects kappa*v_d²=T²/kappa. The spring is explicitly energetic and temperature-independent, and its stored energy is T²/(2*k_s). Thus work minus thermostat heat is the time derivative of stored spring energy. This is a physical attribution error despite exact mechanical response. It is not an omitted mode or an inaccurate approximation to the boundary impedance.

For unit incident amplitude, a=alpha*j/(1-i omega tau). Normalizing by mean incident power 1/2 and writing q(t)=q0+Re[q2 exp(-2i omega t)] gives

```
q0 = |a|²/(alpha*p²)
q2 = a²/(alpha*p²)
w2 = a*j/p² = (1-i omega tau)*q2.
```

The mean source work and true heat are identical at every control and p. Phase quadratures differ at finite contact relaxation. The scalar in-phase and quadrature definitions use cos and sin with the incoming electric field at the front face as the clock. Both ordinary quadratures are included in each diagnostic group; their group RMS has a healthy scale even when one component changes sign. No per-component division near zero or background cancellation is used.

The ideal fast thermostat, dashpot-only thermal measurement, temperature-independent spring and weak-field limit are stated publicly. The material and thermal response are an explicit ideal model, not a claim that every physical fluid surface has this law. Bulk fluid/background heating and rear-contact heat are separate signals. The optical mean-energy check uses the correct bulk integral of gamma*|J|²+nu*|J_z|² divided by p², plus both contact heats. It agrees with 1-|r|²-|t|².

The source's instantaneous mechanical work can be negative, whereas dashpot heat is nonnegative. A solver may repair the model with a simple energy balance or positivity check. This limitation is preserved; scientific separation does not establish model difficulty.

## Independent reference and limits

The reference solves the eight-real first-order Maxwell/momentum boundary problem with collocation instead of modal roots. It then integrates the contact ODE in time. A one-period affine map determines the periodic initial traction, and direct integration of squared dashpot traction gives the mean and Fourier coefficients. It does not insert the analytic second-harmonic correction into the measured heat. The same physical continuum equations are shared; this is independent field and time-domain heat evaluation, not a microscopic surface simulation.

Every one of the 28 graded scalar inputs is independently refined, including four mean anchors. Maximum oracle/reference discrepancy is 5.75e-12; maximum tolerance-refinement change is 5.21e-12. Thirty-two full-domain corners and 32 random interiors include all three readouts and give maximum reference error 5.74e-12. Mean optical energy residual is at most 1.31e-15. The physical waveform identity |q2|=q0, exact mean equivalence and work/heat harmonic identity hold at roundoff. Positive bulk/contact dissipation and causal series-contact response also rule out a real-frequency undriven resonance in this passive lossy system.

The preserved author-only limits include tau0, small nonzero tau with less than 4% discrepancy, weak friction approaching free slip, and strong friction approaching no slip. Both slip extremes suppress the contact heat signal and are not diagnostic cases. The allowed task tau range [.2,1.2] has finite memory. The bounded prototype retains every first screen and the original historical evidence.

Local controls use fresh temporary app/tests copies and the actual public/private pytest files. Oracle passes 7/7 in 1.287 seconds; source passes 4 and fails exactly 3 predictions in 1.301 seconds. Cold graded reference evaluation took .629 seconds. The full author validation took 16.32 seconds. No Docker control or model trial has been run by the author.
