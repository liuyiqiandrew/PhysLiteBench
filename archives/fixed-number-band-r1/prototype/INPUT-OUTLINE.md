# Proposed fixed-number calorimetry apparatus

This is an author-only outline, not a task package or evaluated input.

A macroscopic ideal solid has two flat bands, each containing M spinless one-particle states. Their energies are -W/2 and +W/2. Fermions do not interact, and each one-particle state admits at most one particle. W is independent of temperature and has the same unknown value in every experiment. Geometry and band energies are held fixed during each measurement.

The sample is prepared with exactly N particles and then electrically isolated. Its known filling is n=N/(2M). A weak thermal contact permits complete canonical equilibration, including transitions between bands, while preserving N. Equilibration is taken before the contact energy is neglected. There is no additional preparation constraint on the occupation of either band.

A differential calorimeter measures the reversible heat supplied per temperature increment. The contribution of the empty support and thermal contact is subtracted. Divide the reading by the total number 2M of available one-particle states, and report its thermodynamic limit at fixed n. The number of particles in each sample remains fixed during the temperature change.

Use an energy unit E0, temperature unit E0/kB and heat-capacity unit kB per available one-particle state. The common W/E0 is in [.8,1.2]. Proposed exact controls are temperature in [.1,.6] and filling in [.1,.9]. Different prepared samples can have different fillings. A future calibration dataset would contain repeated readings with fixed instrument uncertainty. No data have been generated here.

The eventual task would request fitting W and predicting this calorimeter reading. It would use the existing neutral instruction permitting replacement of the full implementation. This outline gives no required algorithm or response formula.
