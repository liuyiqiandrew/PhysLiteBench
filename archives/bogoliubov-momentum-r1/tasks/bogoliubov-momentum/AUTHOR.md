# Bogoliubov momentum, revision 1

The task measures the equilibrium variance of a gas's total mechanical momentum, after a bath has prepared the unconstrained Gibbs ensemble. The bath exchanges momentum during preparation; isolation and translation-invariant release then preserve each shot's momentum. It is therefore not a fixed-total-momentum ensemble. The quadratic Hamiltonian is the specified exact ideal model, rather than an invitation to choose a many-body approximation. The condensate is in the zero-momentum mode.

The completed source retains the full Bogoliubov dispersion, mode mixing, quantum depletion and thermal depletion. It subtracts the ground-state depletion before multiplying the remaining particle density by m*T. That extension of the ideal-gas momentum identity is mathematically complete, positive and exactly correct on every calibration setting. It misses the distinction between thermal particles outside the condensate and the correlations that determine their total momentum.

With epsilon=p²/(2m), mu=interaction and E=sqrt(epsilon*(epsilon+2*mu)), the thermal mode population is n=1/(exp(E/T)-1). A momentum-preserving Bogoliubov transformation gives

    N_p - N_(-p) = b_p^dagger*b_p - b_(-p)^dagger*b_(-p).

Consequently the momentum variance per volume is

    Var(P_x)/V = integral_0^infinity p^4*n*(1+n) dp / (6*pi²).

The source instead uses m*T times the integral of p²*(epsilon+mu)*n/E divided by 2*pi². At mu=0, integration by parts converts the exact momentum integral into that expression, for Bose occupations as well as dilute classical occupations. This identity makes all ideal-gas calibration exactly equivalent for every detector gain. At nonzero interaction the mode dispersion and opposite-momentum correlations change the relation. The ground state has quantum depletion but zero total momentum variance.

The independent verifier works in the physical particle basis. Its normal and anomalous Gaussian covariances are A=((epsilon+mu)*coth(E/(2T))/E-1)/2 and M=-mu*coth(E/(2T))/(2E). Wick contraction gives the momentum integrand p^4*[A*(A+1)-M²]/(6*pi²). It combines the pair terms before integrating, preserving the cancellation of opposite quantum-pair momentum. Adaptive quadrature differs from the oracle's mapped Gauss-Legendre rule. A further finite-Fock check diagonalizes the exact two-mode Hamiltonian in conserved number-difference blocks; cutoffs 24, 48, 80 and 120 converge to the Wick variance without invoking the quasiparticle formula.

The verifier and oracle agree within 7e-12 relative over the tested parameter grid. Changing oracle quadrature from 192 to 320 nodes changes results by less than 3.5e-15. The ideal result T*(T/(2*pi))^(3/2)*zeta(3/2), the ground-state depletion mu^(3/2)/(3*pi²), and zero ground-state momentum variance are checked. The low-temperature phonon result is 2*pi²*T^5/(45*mu^(5/2)); numerical ratios to this result approach one as T decreases. The displayed ranges have depletion below 1.5% of the specified condensate density at the checked extreme settings. The exact quadratic model remains the task's definition.
