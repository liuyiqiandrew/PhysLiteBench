# Annular survivor transport: rejected prototype

This is a possible same-family revision of survivor-transport, developed before any r1 model outcome. It is not a task package and has no model evaluations. The tested physical effect is real, but its ordinary Poiseuille drift separation is too small for the unchanged 0.04 gate. No further build is recommended from this screen.

A rotating reflecting inner cylinder and stationary outer cylinder bound an annulus. The outer coating has a positive azimuthally patterned Robin capture velocity. The real Couette flow is u_theta=Omega*a^2*(R^2/r-r)/(R^2-a^2); the ordinary pressure-driven axial annular Poiseuille profile is positive and normalized to unit peak. No background cancellation, tailored axial flow or special detector is used. Uniform injection and selection by survival through the full interval define accumulated axial drift.

Both models solve the full nonreversible transverse generator and its loss rate. The proposed stronger source uses the squared forward principal density, applying the r1 self-adjoint rule to this apparatus. The physical path occupation uses the product of forward density and backward survival mode. The operator is discretized by conservative radial face diffusion, integrated angular Couette/diffusion flux with positive Scharfetter-Gummel rates, and the outer half-cell Robin resistance. Square-root cell-volume coordinates keep all radial measures explicit.

The initial 45-case screen varied three inner radii, five rotation rates and three positive cosine capture patterns. Its largest drift discrepancy was 0.760%. One limited follow-up tested 72 localized capture patterns and complementary low-capture holes with ordinary flow unchanged. The largest follow-up discrepancy converged from 1.313% at24x64 to1.325% at72x192, with positive drift around0.668. Three top settings were refined. Principal tilted-eigenvalue derivatives independently extract the observable on the same discrete generator and agree with the left/right product to about3.1e-11. This checks the observable identity, not independence of two full spatial discretizations.

At the strongest screened configuration, diffusivities .7,1,1.4 and both rotation signs give at most1.324% at48x128, with drift at least0.6657. Nonrotating patterned capture and rotating uniform capture agree with the squared-mode source to roundoff. These are legitimate shared calibration subsets, but the tested hidden signals do not separate at4%. This limited screen is not a theorem excluding all annular geometries; it is enough to reject this proposed minimal apparatus rather than tune the readout or tighten grading.

Files preserve every screen, the three refinement sequences, parameter/reversal checks and exact generating-source hashes. An initial uv launch failed before execution because its default cache was not writable. The successful scientific runs used the existing cached Python environment with NumPy2.3.3/SciPy1.16.3 and bytecode disabled. No model runs, canonical changes or survivor-r1 changes occurred.
