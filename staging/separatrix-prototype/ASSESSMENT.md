# Separatrix capture prototype

The bounded prototype passes. Direct Hamiltonian ensembles agree with the capture-area energy prediction well below a .04 relative prediction gate at practical runtime. This is a recommendation for root and independent peer review, not a task package or a claim about model difficulty.

The Hamiltonian is H=p²/2+q⁴/4-s q²/2+delta s^(3/2)q. Initial J is uniform on [J0,1.2 J0], with independent uniform canonical angle; J0 is the unknown preparation scale. For each finite ramp duration, average final total energy over this finite-width ensemble, then take the slow-ramp limit. The energy zero is exactly that in the Hamiltonian. Negative values are ordinary potential-plus-kinetic energies; no offset or difference of backgrounds is used. Input wording is in INPUT-OUTLINE.md.

Writing q=√s x and p=s P gives energy scaling s² and orbit-area scaling s^(3/2). The two separatrix lobes therefore grow monotonically with constant area fractions alpha_i. An incoming outer orbit with action J meets the separatrix at s_c=[2 pi J/A_total(1)]^(2/3). Its capture probabilities are alpha_i, and its outgoing lobe action is alpha_i J. The source instead preserves the full global enclosed phase volume at one common final energy. That is a coherent ergodic adiabatic continuation; it is physically wrong once disconnected lobes are populated at different energies. Symmetry makes the energy closures coincide when delta=0, so asymmetry is necessary.

Calibration endpoints .10–.20 leave the whole initial action band on connected outer orbits. The two predictions coincide there for every J0. With J=J0 z and z uniform on [1,1.2], d<E>/dJ0=<z omega(J0 z)> is strictly positive. Nine noncrossing control settings, each checked at 17 J0 values, have positive slopes. Seventeen inverse fits recover J0 within 9.30e-13. No noise/calibration dataset was generated.

For delta≤.25 the dimensionless saddle coordinate c satisfies |c|<.27. The lobe-area representation gives a conservative total-area upper bound 5.067 and lower bound 2.38. Thus the separatrix action is below .073 at calibration endpoint .20, while it exceeds .91 at final endpoint1.8. These bounds separate the preparation band [.12,.192] from both limiting regimes throughout the candidate parameter box. The saddle remains nondegenerate, since 1-3c²>.78. The intermediate crossing regime is defined by the same Hamiltonian and is not excluded from the apparatus.

The independent reference directly propagates the full Hamiltonian ensemble. It uses Gauss quadrature over initial action, uniformly spaced orbital-time points for the canonical angle, and a fourth-order extended-phase-space symplectic integrator. Initial period and phase are checked by a separate DOP853 orbit solution. The final mean-energy calculation contains no capture probability or final branch-action substitution. A separate adaptive DOP853 propagation of the same ensemble agrees with symplectic integration within 2.09e-8 relative; changing its tolerance gives a 2.04e-9 relative change. Integrated external work is checked against final-minus-initial energy.

The first eight representative exploratory runs are preserved in exploration.jsonl. They compare durations64–512, action/phase orders, integration step and cubic/quintic ramp shapes. The full study then uses durations256–2048, up to64 action nodes and512 phase points, phase-offset changes and step halving. These are empirical convergence checks, not a proof of a high-precision asymptotic extrapolation; finite-grid error is not monotone in duration.
