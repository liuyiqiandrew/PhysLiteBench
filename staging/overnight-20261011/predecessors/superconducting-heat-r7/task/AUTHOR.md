# Superconducting heat R7: two oscillator modes

The R6 proximity islands, rotating spin barrier, spin relaxation, phase-contrast measurement, calibration, conductance and tolerances are retained. Add an independent thermal phase oscillator at 48 K to the existing 24 K mode. Both modes have the same temperature and weak-cell bath hierarchy, so detailed balance remains thermal and the existing forbidden gap-alignment argument still holds.

For transitions ell and k in the two modes, the environment energy gain is 24*(ell+2k) K. The normal probability is P_ell Q_k and the anomalous covariance is (-1)^(ell+k) P_ell Q_k. Sum over all unobserved oscillator transitions. At a fixed environment energy, different joint transitions can have opposite anomalous sign: for example two quanta of the first mode and one quantum of the second. Consequently the anomalous covariance is a signed convolution and cannot be reconstructed from just the parity of exchanged energy in units of 24 K. Its absolute value is bounded by the normal probability. Since both modes share a temperature, normal and anomalous forward/reverse spectra obey the same detailed balance factor.
