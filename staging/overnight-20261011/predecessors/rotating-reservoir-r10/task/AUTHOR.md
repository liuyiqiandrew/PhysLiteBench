memory, the independent motor, velocity-swap energy conservation, common bath
noise and nonzero third cumulants. It agrees with the apparatus at zero flux
fraction, so calibration cannot distinguish it. Its remaining physical error
is replacing the instantaneous relative-speed hazard by its averaged rate.
