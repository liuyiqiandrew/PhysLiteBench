# Stiff-spring versus rigid trimer: overlap assessment

Do not build this proposal as a new family. No numerical prototype, task package or evaluation was created.

The proposed apparatus would equilibrate a finite Cartesian spring chain at each bond stiffness before taking the stiffness to infinity. The alternative would use the canonical Hamiltonian of the exactly constrained chain. A physically pinned central bead can remove the coupling of the two angular kinetic degrees of freedom, making bending-moment calibration coincide. Releasing that support while preserving the two limiting procedures can distinguish the measures. This is a valid physical ensemble distinction if all support and preparation conditions are explicit.

However, `archives/elastic-ring-neutral-r1` already tests precisely finite-spring equilibration versus the correctly normalized rigid ensemble. Its source computes the exact constrained tangent metric, and its hidden physical weight includes the integrated normal spring fluctuations. That is the proposed missing physics, including the order of equilibrium and stiff limits. The archived rigid-linkage family also covers the complementary exact-holonomic momentum measure. Changing from a four-bead ring to a three-bead chain or strengthening the calibration does not create a new physical mechanism.
