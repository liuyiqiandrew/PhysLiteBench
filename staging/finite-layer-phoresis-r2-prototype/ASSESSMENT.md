# Slip phoresis prototype

This is a proposed substantive revision 2 of finite-layer-phoresis. Revision 1 scored 1/3 genuine physical passes, with two reviewed physical failures; its task and evidence remain in archives/finite-layer-phoresis-r1. It is not a new family or an unchanged retry.

The sphere has a known Navier slip length b. Its impermeable surface imposes tangential relative velocity equal to b/eta times solvent tangential traction, with the normal directed from the sphere into the fluid. The conservative solute potential is purely radial and fixed to the particle; there is no additional surface solute drag. The prior total solution-pressure convention and force-free fluid-traction plus potential-reaction balance are retained.

The supplied approximation would keep the exact curved solute BVP, the full no-slip curved force kernel and the exact Navier-slip translation drag. It treats phoretic thrust as fixed while slip changes drag. The physical force transmission also changes: a translating Navier-slip auxiliary flow has coefficients A=3(1+2beta)/(2(1+3beta)), B=1/(2(1+3beta)), beta=b/a. Its reciprocal-plus-direct-reaction kernel is r^2-A*a*r+B*a^3/r. At the wall it vanishes but its derivative is 3*a*beta/(1+3beta), producing a first-moment contribution absent from a drag-only correction. Sphere preparations with b=0 provide exact calibration equivalence and validate all prior finite-curvature physics.

Independent reference: the archived conservative solute finite elements supply the concentration to a force-free fourth-order Stokes streamfunction BVP. Wall conditions are psi=0 and (1+2b/a)*psi'-b*psi''=0; exterior matching excludes the Stokeslet and the growing r^4 flow. This reference does not use the reciprocal kernel or slip drag formula. Direct integrated fluid traction includes the nonzero-slip term 2*psi'/a^2 and balances the smooth-potential reaction.

The 64-case prototype gives 16.5–79.5% drag-only errors, nonzero velocity/gradient signals at least 0.0123 at eta 1.2, independent FEM/Stokes error below 5.92e-10, force-balance residual below 2.04e-11 and boundary residual below 1.78e-15. Zero-slip calibration equivalence is exact, geometric length rescaling is quadratic, positive slip gives nonnegative interfacial dissipation, and the thin-layer sphere/negative-flat-wall ratio is 0.999604. No model evaluations or packaged task/data have been created.

The broad archive task-name and public/author-text scan found no earlier Navier-slip phoretic task. Primary background remains Marbach, Yoshida and Bocquet, Local and global force balance for diffusiophoretic transport, JFM 892, A6 (2020), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7145454/ . That paper explicitly identifies partial slip as a boundary extension; this prototype's modified kernel and reference are independently derived. Physics reviewer independently confirmed both Navier boundary curvature terms and the auxiliary-flow coefficients.

The initial unscaled concentration solver failed only on a separate width=.0003 thin-limit check outside the proposed public domain. The original numerical form and note are preserved in numerical-check/. The final source rescales that coordinate and factors the identical kernel; no physics or cases changed.
