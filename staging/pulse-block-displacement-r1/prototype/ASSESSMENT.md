# Pulse-induced block displacement: bounded feasibility

This is an unevaluated scientific prototype, not a task package. The proposed failure is using canonical wave momentum in a whole-body mechanical translation balance. The source keeps the exact phase dispersion, group delay, pulse spectrum and residence time. It does not replace group velocity by phase velocity or omit material energy from the optical response.

A homogeneous, freely suspended block has the ideal lossless rest-frame response `epsilon_r(omega)=mu_r(omega)=1+f/(1-omega^2)`. Frequency is measured in units of the known material resonance; `c=1` fixes length/time units. The sole unknown is `f in [.2,.4]`. Both faces adjoin vacuum. Their impedance matches vacuum exactly, so no coatings or unspecified coating forces enter. The positive-frequency pulse amplitude is proportional to `(1-u^2)^4` on `|u|<1`, zero elsewhere, with `u=(omega-center)/width`, center in `[1.5,1.7]` and width in `[.04,.1]`. Its entire support lies above the pole, within `[1.4,1.8]`. This is an asymptotic band-limited scattering preparation, not an exactly time-compact pulse.

The body starts at rest and includes all electric and magnetic material oscillators in its known inertial mass M. It has no external mechanical support or bath during the pulse. Measure the center of mass of the complete body, not an embedded absorber or one surface. The material is mechanically stable with translationally invariant internal forces. At leading order in pulse energy over Mc^2, optical response is that of the resting undeformed block. The requested mechanical coefficient is the final displacement divided by pulse energy, taking this small-energy response before the asymptotic late-time limit. This ordering excludes a secular displacement from any higher-order recoil or acoustic heating. The pulse has no spectral weight at the lossless material pole and leaves no order-energy internal excitation. Elastic energy generated by order-energy forces is higher order.

Calibration measures energy-centroid transit time between the two unperturbed block faces. The diagnostic readout is `Mc^2*DeltaX/E`, in length units. The spectrum is weighted by incident energy, not photon number. No population or stochastic convention is involved.

## Physical and supplied predictions

Write `n=1+f/(1-omega^2)` and `ng=d(omega*n)/domega`. The source's momentum assumption is the canonical polariton value `p_can=n*U/c`. Using the exact residence time `ng*L/c`, its mechanical prediction is

    source = L * energy_average[(1-n)*ng].

This is a correct implementation of that momentum assignment, but it pairs canonical wave momentum with the body's kinetic momentum without the corresponding material contribution. It is not a claim that canonical momentum is generally wrong. In a dispersive material, it must also not be mislabeled as the bare field integral of D cross B.

After the pulse has left, the optical energy is the same as for vacuum propagation but its centroid lags. Conservation of the isolated system's center of energy fixes the whole body's compensating translation. Thus

    physical = L * energy_average[ng-1],
    shared transit calibration = L * energy_average[ng].

Both values of displacement are positive throughout the proposed domain: `0<n<1` while `ng>1`. This avoids the obvious sign reversal of the below-resonance version. Both closures use the same exact optical transit response and hence fit the same parameter. No assertion about an independently measured internal electromagnetic momentum is needed to determine the final whole-body position.

For fixed known pulse and length, transit is `L+f*C`, with `C=L*average[(1+omega^2)/(omega^2-1)^2]>0`. Every single calibration setting identifies f globally; the combined exact least-squares objective is a strictly convex quadratic. The prototype uses eighteen pulse/length settings and recovers all 41 strengths across the interval. A future task could use a fixed transit instrument sigma such as .001 time units, independent of f or response. No data or noise realization has been generated here.

## Independent calculation and results

The reference solves the four actual electric/magnetic field interface equations for every populated Fourier frequency. It then forms incident, transmitted and vacuum waveforms with FFTs, integrates their temporal energy-flux centroids, and obtains the body's displacement from final center-of-energy balance. It never differentiates the refractive index or inserts a group-delay integral. This is an independent Maxwell/temporal computation plus a global conservation law, not an independent microscopic simulation of forces inside the material. That distinction remains a limitation to assess before packaging.

The retained sweep has 1,107 parameter/pulse/length points. Source errors are 29.43–45.55%, with physical coefficients .10895–1.67422 and source coefficients .06446–1.18151. All are ordinary positive readings. The 24 independent temporal checks agree to 1.74e-14 relative; doubling the Fourier time window changes the result by at most 1.40e-14. Energy transmission differs from unity by at most 4.45e-16; reflected amplitude is at most 1.58e-16. Time-window edge energy is below 7.04e-16. Group-index finite differentiation, the vacuum limit and all 41 noiseless fits pass. These checks took 3.54 seconds in the recorded final run.

There is also a continuous-domain bound, not merely a sampled margin. For each frequency the source/physical ratio is `(omega^2-1)/(omega^2+1)+f/(omega^2-1)`. Across the full support and unknown interval it lies between 473/888 and 329/444. Its positive physical-energy-weighted average lies in the same interval, giving a gap of at least 115/444, or25.90%, for every allowed pulse and length. Physical readings are at least .08450 and source readings at least .05219 throughout the larger point-frequency envelope. `analytic-bounds.json` records the values and derivation checks.

An initial author-only diagnostic required both prediction ranges to exceed .1. It failed because the source minimum was .06446, although the physical minimum exceeded .1. The original script and failure record are preserved. The guard was relaxed to .05 after inspecting the unchanged ranges, and the report was moved before assertions so a future failed check retains evidence. No scientific formula, domain, prospective task threshold or calibration changed. This was not a model evaluation.

## Primary sources and remaining scope

Its explicit microscopic force treatment assumes a nonmagnetic medium; the present matched electric/magnetic block uses total center-of-energy conservation instead of importing that restricted force density.
